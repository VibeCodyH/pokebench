About Bob Zimmerman

CQ CQ DX de K7BOB! Welcome to Uncle Bob Zimmerman's amateur radio shack online. Licensed Extra Class since 1979. Currently running 100 watts into an attic G5RV dipole on 20m and 40m CW, plus prepping our county ARES packet radio net for the Y2K rollover!
