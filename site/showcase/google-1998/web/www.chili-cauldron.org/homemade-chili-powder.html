From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 15 min | Yield: 3/4 cup fresh blend

Pre-ground cumin loses 70% of its essential aroma oils within six weeks! Buy a cheap $14 electric blade coffee grinder reserved STRICTLY for chiles and cumin (tell folks at Redwood State University CS Dept too!).


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Whole Cumin Seeds (Toasted)3 Tbsp
Dried Ancho & New Mexico Pods4 anchos + 4 NM (seeded)
Mexican Oregano (Rubbed)2 Tbsp
Garlic Powder & Smoked Paprika1 Tbsp + 2 Tbsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Toast whole cumin seeds in a dry skillet 90 seconds until popping and nutty; cool on a plate.
  2. Snap brittle dried chile skins into small squares (toast 15 sec if humid) and pulse inside spice grinder until brick-red powder.
  3. Blend cooled cumin, chile powder, Mexican oregano, smoked paprika, and garlic powder; seal in an amber glass jar.

Cook's Tip: Clean spice grinder by pulsing a slice of stale white bread to absorb lingering pepper oils!
