Specs: Spice Pantry FAQ | Updated Sept 1998 | Buyer Beware

Wonder why your homemade chili tastes flat compared to restaurant bowls? Open your spice cabinet and sniff that grocery-store tin of paprika from 1993 -- it smells like red cardboard dust!


Supermarket Jar vs. Fresh Mail-Order Spices
SpiceSupermarket RealitySmart Mail-Order Upgrade
Sweet & Smoked PaprikaFades to chalky orange in 8 months under bright store lightsOrder vacuum-sealed 4-oz foil bags of Spanish Pimenton de la Vera
Whole Cumin & CorianderPre-ground jars lose volatile oils before checkoutBuy whole seeds by the half-pound; toast and grind fresh monthly
Dried Whole ChilesCrumbling brittle bags at end of aisleLook for supple, glossy, leathery pods that smell of dried fruit & cocoa!
