Colorado Pork Green Chili Stew with Roasted Poblanos

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 30 min | Simmer: 90 min | Heat: 2.5 Alarms | Serves: 8

Steaming Denver diner classic! Tender braised pork shoulder swimming in velvety roasted poblano & Anaheim green chile gravy, ready to ladle over burritos or mop up with warm flour tortillas.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Pork Shoulder / Boston Butt2.5 lbs (3/4-in cubes)
Roasted Poblanos & Hatch Chiles4 poblanos + 4 Hatch (peeled)
All-Purpose Flour (Thickener)1/3 cup
Chicken Broth & Tomatillos5 cups + 4 chopped
Mexican Oregano, Cumin & Garlic1 Tbsp + 2 tsp + 5 cloves

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Char poblanos and Hatch chiles under broiler until blistered black, steam inside a paper bag 10 min, peel skins, and chop.
  2. Brown seasoned pork cubes in lard/oil; sprinkle flour over meat and cook 3 min stirring constantly to make a toasted roux.
  3. Whisk in hot chicken broth slowly, add roasted chiles, tomatillos, and spices, and simmer 90 min until pork shreds easily!

Cook's Tip: Never skip steaming roasted peppers in a covered bowl or paper bag -- skins slip right off!
