Leftover Hickory-Smoked Brisket Burnt-End Chili

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Simmer: 90 min | Heat: 3 Alarms | Serves: 8

Got two pounds of smoky oak-kissed brisket burnt ends left over from Saturday's backyard cookout? Cube them into a rich dark-beer chili cauldron -- instant blue-ribbon flavor!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Smoked Beef Brisket Burnt Ends2 lbs (1/2-in cubes)
Dark Bock or Porter Beer12-oz bottle
Fire-Roasted Tomatoes & Black Beans28-oz can + 15-oz can
Ancho, Chipotle & Brown Sugar2 Tbsp + 1 pepper + 1 Tbsp
Onions, Jalapenos & Beef Broth1 large + 2 diced + 2 cups

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Sweat diced onion, bell pepper, and jalapenos in 1 Tbsp oil until softened; toast chili spices 1 minute.
  2. Deglaze skillet with dark bock beer, scrape fond, then add diced tomatoes, black beans, broth, and smoked brisket cubes.
  3. Simmer gently 75-90 min uncovered so oak smoke infuses the entire pot without drying out brisket edges!

Cook's Tip: Skip extra salt until the end -- barbecue brisket bark already brings plenty of seasoning!
