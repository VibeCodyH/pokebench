From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 30 min | Simmer: 2 hrs | Heat: 5 ALARMS! | Serves: 8

WEAR LATEX GLOVES!! My signature midnight cook-off scorcher pairs four floral orange habaneros with dark brewed espresso and bittersweet chocolate for smoky heat that sneaks up ten seconds later.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Orange Habaneros (Minced Fine)4 whole pods (keep seeds if brave!)
Ground Chuck & Chorizo2 lbs chuck + 1 lb pork chorizo
Strong Brewed Espresso & Dark Cocoa1 cup coffee + 2 oz 70% chocolate
Fire-Roasted Diced Tomatoes28-oz can
Ancho Powder, Cumin & Chipotle3 Tbsp + 1 Tbsp + 2 peppers

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Put on rubber gloves BEFORE slicing habaneros unless you want burning eyeballs until next Tuesday!
  2. Brown beef and chorizo in heavy cauldron, drain half the grease, and sweat onions, garlic, and minced habaneros 6 min.
  3. Add spices, fire-roasted tomatoes, espresso, and dark chocolate; simmer gently 90-120 min until thick, glossy, and roaring hot.

Cook's Tip: Keep cold whole milk and sour cream nearby -- cold water spreads capsaicin oil around your mouth!
