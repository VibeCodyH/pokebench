Specs: 1998 Reference Chart | Wilbur Scoville Organoleptic Scale (SHU)

From mild sweet bells to the fiery Red Savina Habanero (current 1994-1998 Guinness World Record holder!), know exactly how much capsaicin punch you're tossing into the cauldron.

Pepper VarietyScoville Units (SHU)Flavor & Cauldron Use
