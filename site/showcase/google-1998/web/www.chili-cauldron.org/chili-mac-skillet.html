From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 5 min | Cook: 20 min | One Pot | Serves: 4

Too tired after debugging Perl scripts to wait two hours? Elbow macaroni boils directly inside savory spiced beef & tomato broth in one skillet -- starch thickens the sauce automatically!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Ground Beef (85/15)1 lb
Dry Elbow Macaroni2 cups (uncooked!)
Beef Broth & Diced Tomatoes2.5 cups + 15-oz can
Chili Powder, Cumin & Garlic2 Tbsp + 1 tsp + 3 cloves
Shredded Sharp Cheddar1.5 cups

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Brown beef with diced onion in a deep 12-inch skillet; drain half the fat and stir in garlic, chili powder, and cumin.
  2. Pour in beef broth, diced tomatoes, and DRY macaroni elbows; cover and simmer 12-14 min stirring twice until tender.
  3. Turn off heat, fold in shredded cheddar until gooey, and eat straight from the skillet!

Cook's Tip: Top with crushed corn chips and pickled jalapenos for instant dorm gourmet.
