Baked Ranch & Cayenne Oyster Crackers

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 5 min | Bake: 18 min | Oven: 250F | Yield: 1 large bag

Crackers so addictive your guests will empty the bowl before the chili even hits the table! Shake plain oyster crackers in cayenne-spiked ranch oil and crisp low in the oven.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Plain Oyster Crackers16-oz bag (or 2 sleeves saltines)
Neutral Canola or Corn Oil1/2 cup
Dry Buttermilk Ranch Seasoning1-oz packet
Cayenne Pepper & Garlic Powder1 tsp cayenne + 1/2 tsp garlic

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Whisk oil, ranch packet, cayenne, garlic powder, and 1/2 tsp dried dill in a giant mixing bowl.
  2. Toss oyster crackers gently for 3 minutes until every cracker glistens evenly without crushing.
  3. Spread single layer on two rimmed baking sheets and bake at 250F for 18 min, stirring halfway; cool completely!

Cook's Tip: Store cooled crackers in an airtight Tupperware tub up to three weeks (if they last 24 hours!).
