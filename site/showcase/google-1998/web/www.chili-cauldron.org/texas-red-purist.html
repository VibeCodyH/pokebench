Authentic No-Bean Texas Bowl of Red (Dried Ancho & Guajillo)

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 35 min | Simmer: 2.5 hrs | Heat: 3 Alarms | Serves: 8

CASI Purist Manifesto: Beans are a fine side dish, folks, NEVER inside an authentic Bowl of Red! We blend toasted dried ancho and guajillo chiles into velvet paste to braise hand-cubed beef chuck.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Beef Chuck Roast (1/2-in cubes)3.5 lbs
Dried Ancho & Guajillo Chiles4 anchos + 4 guajillos (seeded)
Beef Broth & Masa Harina4 cups broth + 3 Tbsp masa
Cumin, Mexican Oregano & Garlic2 Tbsp + 1 Tbsp + 6 cloves
Lard or Bacon Fat3 Tbsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Toast stemmed dried chiles 20 sec in dry skillet, steep 20 min in boiling water, and blend silky smooth with 1 cup soaking liquid.
  2. Brown chuck cubes in hot bacon fat in batches; sweat onions and garlic, toast ground cumin, then pour in red chile puree and beef broth.
  3. Simmer uncovered 2 hours until beef melts; whisk masa harina with warm water and stir in last 15 min for glossy brick-red body.

Cook's Tip: Serve with hot salted crackers or Aunt Clara's zero-sugar skillet cornbread -- NO kidney beans allowed!
