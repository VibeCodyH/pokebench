From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Slow Cook: 6 hrs LOW | Heat: 3 Alarms | Feeds: 12 Hackers

Built to fuel twelve Redwood CS students compiling Linux kernels past 4AM! One giant 6-quart Crock-Pot simmering quietly on the breakroom counter turns $16 of groceries into heroic brain food.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Lean Ground Chuck4 lbs (browned first!)
Pinto, Kidney & Black Beans4 cans total (drained)
Diced Tomatoes & Tomato Sauce2x 28-oz cans + 15-oz can
Chili Powder, Cumin & Cayenne1/3 cup + 2 Tbsp + 1.5 tsp
Red Onions, Garlic & Dark Beer2 large + 6 cloves + 12-oz bottle

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Brown all 4 lbs ground chuck in a large skillet in two batches; drain excess fat so crockpot soup never gets greasy.
  2. Dump browned beef, chopped onions, garlic, tomatoes, beans, spices, and dark stout beer into a 6-quart slow cooker.
  3. Stir well, set lid on LOW for 6-8 hours (or HIGH 3.5 hrs), and drop paper bowls + shredded cheddar beside the keyboard!

Cook's Tip: Set a sticky note on the Crock-Pot dial: DO NOT UNPLUG FOR MORE MONITOR POWER!
