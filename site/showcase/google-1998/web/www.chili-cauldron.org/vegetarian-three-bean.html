Smoky Chipotle & Black Bean Dorm-Friendly Chili

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 15 min | Simmer: 40 min | Heat: 2.5 Alarms | Serves: 6

Who says meatless chili tastes thin? Roasted sweet potato cubes add natural creaminess while smoky canned chipotles in adobo bring deep campfire barbecue backbone on a single dorm hot-plate!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Black & Kidney Beans3 cans (15 oz, rinsed)
Diced Sweet Potatoes1.5 lbs (1/2-in cubes)
Chipotles in Adobo Sauce2 peppers + 2 Tbsp adobo
Crushed Tomatoes & Corn28-oz can + 1 cup kernels
Smoked Paprika, Cumin & Cocoa1 Tbsp + 1 Tbsp + 1 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Saute diced onion, bell pepper, and sweet potato cubes in olive oil 8 min until edges begin caramelizing.
  2. Stir in minced chipotles, adobo sauce, cumin, and smoked paprika for 60 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes, rinsed beans, and 1.5 cups water; simmer 30 min until sweet potatoes are tender and corn pops sweet.

Cook's Tip: Leftovers taste twice as smoky the next afternoon -- pack in a thermos for campus lunch!
