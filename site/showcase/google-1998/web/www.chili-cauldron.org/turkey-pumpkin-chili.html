Autumn Harvest Ground Turkey & Pumpkin Ale Chili

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 15 min | Simmer: 45 min | Heat: 2 Alarms | Serves: 6

Crisp October leaves outside call for hearty ground turkey chili enriched with canned solid-pack pumpkin puree (adds silkiness, NOT pie sweetness!) and malty autumn pumpkin ale.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Ground Turkey Thigh (93/7)2 lbs
Solid-Pack Pumpkin Puree1 can (15 oz, NOT pie mix!)
Autumn Pumpkin Ale & Broth12-oz bottle + 1.5 cups
White & Black Beans2 cans (rinsed)
Smoked Paprika, Sage & Chipotle1 Tbsp + 1/2 tsp + 1 pepper

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Brown ground turkey with onion, bell pepper, garlic, sage, cumin, and smoked paprika.
  2. Deglaze browned fond with pumpkin ale, then stir in pumpkin puree, chicken broth, diced tomatoes, and beans.
  3. Simmer gently 30 minutes until rich orange-rust sauce clings to every spoonful!

Cook's Tip: Garnish with toasted salted pepitas (pumpkin seeds) and sour cream swirls!
