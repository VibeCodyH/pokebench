Jalapeno Cheddar Skillet Cornbread & Honey Butter

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 12 min | Bake: 22 min | Oven: 400F | Serves: 8

Golden skillet cornbread studded with sharp cheddar, sweet creamed corn, and pickled jalapeno coins -- served sizzling with whipped honey butter!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Yellow Cornmeal & Flour1 cup each
Cream-Style Corn & Buttermilk1 cup + 3/4 cup
Sharp Cheddar & Pickled Jalapenos1.5 cups + 1/3 cup chopped
Eggs, Butter & Honey2 eggs + 4 Tbsp butter + 2 Tbsp honey

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Heat 2 Tbsp butter in 9-inch cast-iron pan inside 400F oven until bubbling.
  2. Stir dry ingredients, whisk in buttermilk, creamed corn, eggs, remaining melted butter, cheese, and drained jalapenos.
  3. Pour into hot skillet and bake 20-24 min until crisp browned edges pull slightly away from iron.

Cook's Tip: Whip 4 Tbsp softened salted butter with 2 Tbsp warm clover honey to slather on hot squares!
