Verde Hatch Chile & White Bean Chicken Chili

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 25 min | Simmer: 50 min | Heat: 2 Alarms | Serves: 6

Bright, citrusy New Mexico comfort: charred green Hatch chiles and roasted tomatillos simmered with shredded chicken thighs, cumin, and creamy Great Northern white beans.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Roasted Hatch or Anaheim Chiles6 large (peeled, chopped)
Husked Tomatillos & Jalapenos1 lb + 2 seeded jalapenos
Chicken Thighs (Seared & Shredded)2 lbs
Great Northern White Beans2 cans (15 oz, rinsed)
Cilantro, Lime & Monterey Jack1/2 cup + 1 lime + 1 cup cheese

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Broil tomatillos and jalapenos 7 min until blistered; puree in blender with cilantro and garlic.
  2. Saute onions with cumin and coriander, pour in green tomatillo sauce, chicken broth, chopped Hatch chiles, and chicken thighs.
  3. Simmer 35 min, shred chicken, mash 1/2 cup of the white beans to thicken broth naturally, and finish with fresh lime juice.

Cook's Tip: Garnish with diced avocado, crunchy radish slices, and crushed tortilla chips!
