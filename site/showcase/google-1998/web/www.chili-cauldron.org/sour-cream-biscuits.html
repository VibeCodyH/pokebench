Drop Cheddar & Chive Biscuits for Chili Dipping

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 10 min | Bake: 14 min | Oven: 425F | Yield: 12 biscuits

No rolling pin, no pastry cutter, no flour mess on the counter -- just stir ice-cold buttermilk into grated butter, sharp cheddar, and fresh chives, then scoop bumpy mounds onto parchment!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
All-Purpose Flour & Baking Powder2 cups + 1 Tbsp
Frozen Salted Butter (Grated!)1/2 cup (1 stick)
Cold Buttermilk1 cup
Sharp Cheddar & Fresh Chives1 cup shredded + 3 Tbsp snipped
Garlic Butter Brush2 Tbsp melted + 1/4 tsp garlic powder

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Grate frozen stick of butter on box grater directly into flour, baking powder, and 1/2 tsp salt; toss lightly.
  2. Stir in shredded cheddar, chives, and cold buttermilk just until shaggy dough forms (do not overmix!).
  3. Drop 12 heaping mounds onto parchment sheet, bake 13-15 min at 425F, and brush hot tops with melted garlic butter!

Cook's Tip: Grating frozen butter makes flaky pockets faster than any pastry blender!
