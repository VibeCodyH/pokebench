Hosting a neighborhood or office chili cook-off? Print our blind-tasting judging sheet so nobody gets disqualified by mystery garnishes or watery soup!


Annual Chili Cauldron 100-Point Rubric
CriterionMax PointsWhat Blind Judges Look For
Aroma10 ptsInviting roasted cumin, toasted chile, savory beef smell -- zero scorched bitterness
Consistency20 ptsRich stew-like suspension; meat doesn't sink in thin tomato water
Red Color10 ptsDeep appetizing brick mahogany; no dull gray grease slick on top
Taste & Balance40 ptsComplex layers of chile sweetness, smoke, acid, and salt (not just salt-lick!)
Aftertaste / Heat20 ptsPleasant lingering warmth -- 5-alarm burn shouldn't erase all flavor!
