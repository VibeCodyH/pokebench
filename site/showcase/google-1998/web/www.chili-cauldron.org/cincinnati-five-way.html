Cinnamon & Clove Cincinnati Chili Over Spaghetti

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Simmer: 90 min | Heat: 1.5 Alarms | Serves: 6

Ohio's famous Greek-spiced meat sauce! Never brown the ground beef -- simmer it gently in cold water with cinnamon, allspice, cloves, and cocoa until velvety fine, then pile over spaghetti.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Lean Ground Beef (Unbrowned!)2 lbs
Water & Tomato Paste4 cups + 6-oz can
Cinnamon, Allspice & Cloves1.5 tsp + 1/2 tsp + 1/4 tsp
Unsweetened Cocoa & Cider Vinegar1 Tbsp + 2 Tbsp
Spaghetti & Mild Finely Grated Cheddar1 lb + 3 cups (fluffy mound!)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Crumble raw beef into cold water in pot and mash with a potato masher until completely fine-textured before turning burner on.
  2. Add onion, garlic, tomato paste, cider vinegar, cocoa, chili powder, cinnamon, allspice, and cloves; simmer uncovered 90 min.
  3. Serve 'Five-Way': spaghetti base, hot Cincinnati chili, simmered red kidney beans, raw white onion, and a mountain of finely shredded cheddar!

Cook's Tip: Chill the chili overnight and lift off the solid top fat cap for authentic parlor texture!
