From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 10 min | Steep: 20 min | Essential Technique

Ditch dusty supermarket 'chili powder' jars that sat on shelves since 1995! Real chili heads toast whole dried New Mexico, Pasilla, and Mulato pods until puffed and fragrant.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Whole Dried Pods (Stemmed)8-10 pods (mix sweet & hot)
Boiling Kettle Water3 cups
Dry Cast-Iron SkilletMed-low heat

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Wipe dust off dried pods with a damp cloth, snap off stems, and shake loose seeds into a jar (save good ones for spring planting!).
  2. Press opened pods flat onto a dry medium-hot skillet with a spatula 15-20 seconds per side until pliable and nutty -- NEVER burn black or they turn bitter!
  3. Submerge toasted pods in boiling water under a small saucer 20 min, then puree with fresh garlic in blender and strain smooth.

Cook's Tip: Strain puree through a coarse wire mesh sieve to catch tough skin flakes for restaurant-velvet chili.
