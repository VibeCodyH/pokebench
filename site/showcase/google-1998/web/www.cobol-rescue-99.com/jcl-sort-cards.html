Fixing DFSORT Year-Two-Digit Collation in Nightly Batch JCL

Even after updating COBOL paragraphs, nightly MVS batch jobs crash when DFSORT or SyncSort sorts 000103 (Jan 3, 2000) ahead of 991231 (Dec 31, 1999). Apply IBM PTFs enabling Y2T century-windowed sort formats directly inside SYSIN cards:

//STEP020  EXEC PGM=SORT
//SYSIN    DD *
  OPTION Y2PAST=1950
  SORT FIELDS=(15,6,Y2T,A)   /* REPLACES OLD 15,6,CH,A */
