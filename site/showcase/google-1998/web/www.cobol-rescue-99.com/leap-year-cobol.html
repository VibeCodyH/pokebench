Writing a Foolproof Leap Year Paragraph in COBOL-85

Never hardcode DIVIDE WS-YEAR BY 4 GIVING Q REMAINDER R alone! Use the full Gregorian 400-year check so Tuesday, February 29, 2000 processes cleanly without interest-accrual errors:

CHECK-LEAP-YEAR.
    MOVE "N" TO WS-LEAP-FLAG
    DIVIDE WS-YYYY BY 400 GIVING Q REMAINDER R400
    DIVIDE WS-YYYY BY 100 GIVING Q REMAINDER R100
    DIVIDE WS-YYYY BY   4 GIVING Q REMAINDER R4
    IF R400 = 0 OR (R4 = 0 AND R100 NOT = 0)
        MOVE "Y" TO WS-LEAP-FLAG
    END-IF.
