Pivot Year Windowing (1950-2049) vs 8-Byte Field Expansion in DB2

With fewer than 16 months left before January 2000, full VSAM/DB2 database expansion is impossible for many Fortune 500 shops. Compare the two dominant remediation strategies:

CriterionPivot Windowing (1950-2049)8-Byte Field Expansion
File / DB2 SchemaUnchanged (PIC 9(6) kept)Altered (CHAR(8) / DB2 DATE)
Remediation Effort1x (logic branches only)4x - 6x (JCL, copybooks, bridges)
LongevityFails for birthyears < 1950!Permanent through year 9999
