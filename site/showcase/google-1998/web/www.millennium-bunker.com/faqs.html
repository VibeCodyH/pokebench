Millennium Bunker Shipping Delays & Bulk Freight FAQ

Q: Why does a pallet of rice take 3-4 weeks to arrive via Yellow Freight?
A: Because we ship real 1,200-lb food and water-drum pallets sustainably by LTL motor freight, unlike overnight dog food delivery dot-coms who lose money flying 50-lb parcels overnight to inflate IPO slide decks!

Q: Can I pick up in person at your Bend, Oregon warehouse?
A: Yes -- Saturdays 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM with confirmed order number. Bring a pickup truck or utility trailer.

Q: Do you share customer mailing lists?
A: Never. Order invoices are shredded after shipment confirmation.
