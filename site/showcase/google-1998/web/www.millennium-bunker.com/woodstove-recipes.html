Cast Iron Dutch Oven & Woodstove Sourdough Bread Without Power

Your Panasonic bread machine goes silent if the transformer blows -- but a seasoned #12 Lodge cast iron Dutch oven over hardwood coals or a woodstove top bakes golden sourdough boules with crispy blistered crusts! Start learning live-culture starters now from Aunt Clara's 1978 Sourdough Starter & San Francisco Loaf Guide and Hearthside Recipes (or heat a pot of The Five-Alarm Chili Cauldron):

WOODSTOVE SOURDOUGH (1 LOAF):
  3 cups bread flour | 1 cup active starter | 1 cup warm well water | 1.5 tsp salt
  Knead 8 min; rise 4 hrs covered; place on greased Dutch oven bottom.
  Coals: 8 briquettes/hardwood embers under, 16 on flanged lid (~38 min).
