Hand-Crank Shortwave & Amateur Radio Emergency Frequencies

When local AM/FM stations lose studio-to-transmitter links, shortwave receivers (Grundig Yacht Boy / BayGen Freeplay crank radio) pull BBC World Service, VOA, and regional FEMA/ARES amateur nets off battery or hand crank:
