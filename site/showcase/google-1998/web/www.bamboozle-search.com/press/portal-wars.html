Bamboozle Unveils 47 New Links on Its Homepage to Fight Yahoo!

SAN FRANCISCO -- Sept. 13, 1998 -- Bamboozle Media Corp. today rolled out Portal Redesign 4.2, cramming 47 brand-new channel links, 3 nested stock ticker frames, a mortgage calculator, and horoscope widgets onto our 800x600 front door.

"Users do not want a sterile white page with one search box," declared Bamboozle VP of Stickiness Brad Dawson. "They want 19 channels, classifieds, sports scores, and weather forecasts before scrolling!"
