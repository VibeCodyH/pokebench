PRESS RELEASE: $42 Million Series B Fuels Free Heavy-Parcel Delivery

OAKLAND, Calif. -- Sept. 12, 1998 -- PetChow Express Corp. today closed $42 million in Series B financing led by Webvana Holdings and Sand Hill syndicate funds. The fresh capital enables PetChow Express to expand its signature zero-shipping-fee guarantee to 50-lb kibble sacks, 45-lb litter pails, and 60-gallon glass aquariums nationwide.
