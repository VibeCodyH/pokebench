Cinnamon Pecan Sour Cream Coffee Cake

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Bake: 45 min | Oven: 350F | Serves: 12

No pot of drip coffee is complete without a thick ribbon of cinnamon-pecan streusel running right through the middle of tender sour cream cake!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Butter, Sugar & Sour Cream1/2 cup + 1 cup + 1 cup
Flour, Baking Powder & Soda2 cups + 1 tsp + 1 tsp
Eggs & Vanilla2 large + 1 tsp
Brown Sugar, Pecans & Cinnamon3/4 cup + 1 cup chopped + 2 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Mix brown sugar, chopped pecans, cinnamon, and 2 Tbsp flour in small bowl for streusel ribbon and crown.
  2. Cream butter and sugar fluffy, beat in eggs and vanilla, then alternate folding dry ingredients and sour cream.
  3. Spread half the batter in greased 9-inch Bundt or tube pan, sprinkle half the pecan streusel, repeat, and bake 45 min at 350F.

Cook's Tip: Drizzle thin powdered-sugar milk glaze over the warm cake five minutes after turning out onto platter.
