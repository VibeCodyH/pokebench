25-Minute Weeknight Beef Stroganoff Over Egg Noodles

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min | Stovetop | Serves: 4

Fast 25-minute weeknight supper with velvety sour-cream mushroom pan sauce ladled over hot buttered egg noodles. Remember to pull the pan off heat before stirring in sour cream so it never curdles! Listed on WebGuide '98 Directory.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Beef Sirloin Steak (Thin Strips)1.25 lbs
Cremini Mushrooms & Onion12 oz sliced + 1 small diced
Beef Stock & Dijon Mustard1 cup + 1 Tbsp
Full-Fat Sour Cream & Flour3/4 cup + 2 Tbsp
Wide Egg Noodles10 oz (boiled)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Flash-sear dry salted sirloin strips in hot buttered skillet 2 min until browned; transfer beef to warm plate.
  2. Brown mushrooms and onions 6 min in same pan, dust with flour 1 min, then whisk in beef stock and Dijon until glossy.
  3. Take skillet OFF burner, whisk in sour cream gently, fold beef and juices back in, and ladle immediately over egg noodles.

Cook's Tip: Never boil sour cream once added! A splash of Worcestershire sauce deepens the savory beef flavor.
