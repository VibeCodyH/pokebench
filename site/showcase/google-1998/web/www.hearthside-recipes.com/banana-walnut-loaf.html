Moist Sour Cream Banana Walnut Quick Bread

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 15 min | Bake: 55 min | Oven: 350F | Yield: 1 9x5 loaf

Never throw away speckled black bananas! Mash three super-ripe bananas with tangy full-fat sour cream and toasted California walnuts for a moist loaf that stays tender four days.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Very Ripe Mashed Bananas1.5 cups (3 large)
Full-Fat Sour Cream1/2 cup
Melted Butter & Brown Sugar1/3 cup + 3/4 cup
Flour, Baking Soda & Cinnamon1.75 cups + 1 tsp + 1/2 tsp
Toasted Chopped Walnuts3/4 cup

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Toast walnut pieces in a dry skillet 4 minutes until fragrant; cool completely.
  2. Whisk mashed bananas, melted butter, brown sugar, 1 beaten egg, vanilla, and sour cream; gently fold in dry ingredients and walnuts.
  3. Pour into a parchment-lined 9x5 loaf pan and bake at 350F for 52-58 min until a toothpick comes out clean.

Cook's Tip: Slice thick and toast lightly in a buttered frying pan for breakfast!
