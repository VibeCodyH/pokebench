Sharp Cheddar & Mustard Cracker-Crumb Baked Macaroni

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Bake: 30 min | Oven: 375F | Serves: 8

No boxed orange powder allowed! Build a real stove-top Mornay cheese sauce with 2-year aged extra-sharp cheddar and half a teaspoon of Coleman's dry mustard powder under buttery crushed crackers.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Elbow Macaroni1 lb (boiled shy of al dente)
Butter & Flour (Roux)4 Tbsp + 1/4 cup
Whole Milk (Warmed)3.5 cups
2-Yr Extra Sharp Cheddar & Gruyere12 oz + 4 oz shredded
Buttered Crushed Crackers & Dry Mustard1.5 cups Ritz + 1/2 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Cook butter and flour 2 min, whisk in warm milk gradually, and simmer 5 min until sauce coats the back of a spoon.
  2. Remove from heat and melt in hand-shredded cheddar, gruyere, dry mustard, cayenne pinch, and salt; stir in elbows.
  3. Pour into buttered 9x13 dish, blanket with butter-tossed crushed crackers, and bake 30 min at 375F until golden crisp.

Cook's Tip: Shred block cheese on a box grater yourself -- pre-shredded bagged cheese has anti-caking starch that turns grainy!
