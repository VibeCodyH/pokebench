Three-Cheese Meat Sauce Lasagna (Freezer Friendly)

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 40 min | Bake: 45 min | Oven: 375F | Serves: 10

Big Sunday pan built to feed hungry teenagers or wrap in foil for the chest freezer! Simmer sweet Italian sausage in crushed tomatoes before layering three cheeses.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Sweet Italian Sausage & Ground Chuck1 lb each (browned)
Crushed Tomatoes & Paste2x 28-oz cans + 2 Tbsp paste
Lasagna Noodles (Boiled)1 lb box
Whole-Milk Ricotta + 1 Egg15-oz tub + 1 egg + parsley
Mozzarella & Parmigiano16 oz shredded + 1 cup grated

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Brown sausage and beef with chopped onion and garlic; add crushed tomatoes, paste, oregano, and simmer uncovered 30 min.
  2. Mix ricotta with 1 beaten egg, 1/2 cup parmesan, and dried parsley.
  3. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta, mozzarella three times in deep 9x13 pan; cover with tented foil and bake 25 min + 20 min uncovered at 375F.

Cook's Tip: Let lasagna rest 20 full minutes on the counter after baking so neat slices hold their layers!
