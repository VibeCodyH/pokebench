From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 25 min | Chill: 4 hrs | No Bake | Serves: 10

Welcome to the retro Midwest potluck hall of fame! From savory emerald-green 1958 Lime Perfection Salad to sweet-salty Strawberry Pretzel Salad, learn the secret cream-cheese edge seal.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Crushed Pretzels, Butter & Sugar2 cups + 3/4 cup melted + 3 Tbsp
Cream Cheese & Whipped Topping8 oz softened + 8 oz tub
Strawberry or Lime Gelatin6-oz box + 2 cups boiling water
Sliced Strawberries OR Cabbage/Pimento16 oz berries (for Pretzel version)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Press buttered sugary pretzel crumbs into 9x13 dish and bake 10 min at 350F; cool completely!
  2. Beat softened cream cheese with 1 cup sugar and fold in whipped topping; spread CAREFULLY edge-to-edge touching glass walls to seal crust.
  3. Dissolve Jell-O in boiling water, stir in fruit until syrupy cool, gently spoon over sealed cream cheese, and chill 4 hours.

Cook's Tip: If even a tiny gap sits between cream cheese and pan glass, red Jell-O leaks under and softens the pretzels!
