Crisp Kosher Dill Refrigerator Pickles & Garlic

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Chill: 48 hrs | No Canning | Yield: 2 quarts

Too hot in September to boil a big canning water bath? Pack crisp Kirby pickling cucumbers with smashed garlic and fresh dill umbrellas into clean Mason jars for snappy ice-cold refrigerator pickles!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Fresh Kirby Cucumbers2 lbs (ends trimmed, quartered)
Distilled White Vinegar & Water2 cups each (1:1 brine)
Kosher or Pickling Salt3 Tbsp
Garlic Cloves, Dill & Mustard Seed8 cloves + 4 dill heads + 2 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Trim 1/16 inch off blossom end of every cucumber (blossom enzymes make pickles soggy!).
  2. Boil vinegar, water, salt, and 1 Tbsp sugar 2 min until salt dissolves completely; cool 10 minutes.
  3. Pack cucumber spears tightly into quart jars with garlic, dill, peppercorns, and mustard seed; cover with brine and chill 48 hrs.

Cook's Tip: Add 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes per jar for fiery garlic-dill sandwich spears!
