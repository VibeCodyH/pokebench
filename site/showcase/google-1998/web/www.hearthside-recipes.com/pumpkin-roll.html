Spiced Pumpkin Cream Cheese Roll for Autumn

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 25 min | Bake: 13 min | Oven: 375F | Yield: 10 slices

Showstopper autumn dessert that looks intimidating until you learn the warm tea-towel rolling trick while the spiced pumpkin sponge cake is fresh out of the oven!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Canned Pumpkin Puree2/3 cup
Eggs, Sugar & Flour3 large + 1 cup + 3/4 cup
Pumpkin Pie Spice & Soda2 tsp + 1 tsp
Cream Cheese, Butter & Powdered Sugar8 oz + 6 Tbsp + 1 cup

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Beat eggs and sugar thick, stir in pumpkin, then fold in flour, soda, and spice; spread evenly in parchment-lined 10x15 jelly-roll pan.
  2. Bake 13 min at 375F; flip hot cake onto a clean cotton towel dusted heavily with powdered sugar, peel parchment, and roll tightly inside towel to cool!
  3. Unroll cooled cake, spread whipped cream-cheese vanilla filling edge-to-edge, re-roll snugly without towel, chill 2 hrs, and slice.

Cook's Tip: Roll from the narrow 10-inch short side for extra-thick spiral swirls in every slice!
