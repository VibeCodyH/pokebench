Sky-High Mile-High Lemon Meringue Pie

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 35 min | Bake: 15 min | Oven: 350F | Serves: 8

Never suffer rubbery weeping meringue again! Spread hot fluffy Swiss-style meringue straight onto HOT tangy lemon curd so both layers bond seamlessly without sliding.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Baked 9-inch Pie Shell1 fully pre-baked crust
Fresh Lemon Juice & Zest1/2 cup juice + 2 Tbsp zest
Sugar, Cornstarch & Water1 cup + 1/3 cup + 1.5 cups
Egg Yolks (Filling) / Whites (Top)4 yolks / 4 whites + 1/2 tsp cream of tartar

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Whisk 1 cup sugar, cornstarch, pinch of salt, and water in saucepan until glossy thick; temper yolks in slowly and boil 1 min with lemon juice and butter.
  2. Beat 4 room-temp egg whites + cream of tartar to soft foam, gradually whipping in 6 Tbsp sugar until stiff glossy peaks form.
  3. Pour hot curd into baked crust, immediately pile meringue edge-to-edge touching crust rim, and bake 14 min at 350F until toasted peaks.

Cook's Tip: Chill 4 hours completely before slicing with a wet knife dipped in hot water between cuts!
