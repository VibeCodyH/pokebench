Retro Classic Tuna Noodle Casserole with Crushed Potato Chips

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 15 min | Bake: 30 min | Oven: 375F | Serves: 6

Forget snooty restaurant menus -- nothing cures a rainy Tuesday evening faster than bubbling egg noodles, sweet green peas, white albacore tuna, and a loud crunch of Kettle-cooked potato chips.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Wide Egg Noodles12 oz (boiled al dente)
Solid White Tuna2 cans (6 oz each, drained)
Condensed Cream of Mushroom2 cans + 1/2 cup milk
Frozen Peas & Sharp Cheddar1 cup peas + 1.5 cups shredded
Crushed Potato Chips2 cups (plus 2 Tbsp butter)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Boil egg noodles 2 minutes short of package time so they finish soaking up sauce in the oven without turning mushy.
  2. Fold noodles, mushroom soup, milk, flaked tuna, thawed peas, 1 cup cheddar, and black pepper into a buttered 9x13 dish.
  3. Top with remaining cheese and crushed rippled potato chips; bake uncovered at 375F for 28-30 minutes until bubbling.

Cook's Tip: Add 1 tsp fresh lemon juice and a pinch of dry dill weed to brighten the rich mushroom sauce.
