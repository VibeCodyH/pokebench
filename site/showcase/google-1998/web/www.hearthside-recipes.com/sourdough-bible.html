From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 25 min | Rise: 14 hrs | Oven: 450F | Yield: 2 crusty loaves

Meet 'Bessie', my 1978 stoneware crock sourdough starter born in San Francisco and fed every Saturday morning for twenty years! Keep her bubbly with unbleached flour and lukewarm 80F water.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Active 1978 Starter1 cup (fed 6 hrs prior)
Unbleached Bread Flour5 cups
Filtered Water (80F)2 cups
Kosher Salt2 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Whisk starter and lukewarm water in a stoneware bowl until milky; stir in flour and rest 30 min (autolyse).
  2. Pinch in kosher salt, knead 8 min until smooth, and bulk-ferment 4 hrs with three gentle folds.
  3. Shape into two taut rounds, proof overnight in floured bannetons, and bake inside a preheated 450F Dutch oven 20 min covered + 22 min uncovered.

Cook's Tip: Never toss starter discard down the kitchen drain -- mix with melted butter and fry crisp sourdough griddle cakes!
