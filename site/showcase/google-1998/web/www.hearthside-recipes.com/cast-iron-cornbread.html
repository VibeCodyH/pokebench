Skillet Buttermilk Cornbread (Zero Sugar Southern Style)

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 10 min | Bake: 22 min | Oven: 425F | Serves: 8 wedges

Zero sugar, zero cake-mix fluff -- real Southern-style skillet cornbread baked in a screaming-hot 10-inch Lodge cast iron pan slick with bacon drippings so the bottom crust fries crisp!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Stone-Ground Yellow Cornmeal1.5 cups
All-Purpose Flour1/2 cup
Baking Powder / Soda / Salt2 tsp / 1/2 tsp / 1 tsp
Cultured Buttermilk & Eggs1.5 cups + 2 large eggs
Bacon Drippings or Butter3 Tbsp melted in skillet

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Slide empty cast-iron skillet with bacon drippings into a 425F oven for 10 minutes until shimmering hot.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients in a bowl, then stir in buttermilk and beaten eggs just until moistened (lumps are fine!).
  3. Swirl hot drippings up skillet sides, pour batter into the sizzling iron, and bake 20-24 min until golden crown springs back.

Cook's Tip: Pairs ideally alongside five-alarm chili -- slice hot wedges straight from the pan and pass salted butter.
