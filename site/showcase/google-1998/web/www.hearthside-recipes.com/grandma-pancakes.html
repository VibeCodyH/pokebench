Fluffy Buttermilk Griddle Pancakes & Maple Butter

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 12 min | Cook: 15 min | Griddle: 375F | Yield: 14 pancakes

The Saturday morning secret is separating your eggs and whipping the whites into soft peaks before folding them gently into tangy buttermilk batter!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
All-Purpose Flour2 cups
Baking Powder / Soda / Salt2 tsp / 1 tsp / 1/2 tsp
Cultured Buttermilk2 cups
Eggs (Separated)2 large
Melted Butter & Sugar3 Tbsp butter + 2 Tbsp sugar

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, soda, and salt; stir together buttermilk, egg yolks, and cooled melted butter.
  2. Beat egg whites with clean beaters until soft snowy peaks hold, then fold gently into the lumpy batter in two batches.
  3. Ladle 1/3 cup batter onto lightly buttered 375F griddle; flip once bubbles pop and edges turn matte (~2 min per side).

Cook's Tip: Whip softened butter with 3 Tbsp warm pure maple syrup for instant pancake-house maple butter!
