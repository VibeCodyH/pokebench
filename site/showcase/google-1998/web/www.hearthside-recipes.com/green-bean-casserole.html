Holiday Green Bean Casserole & Crispy Fried Onions

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 15 min | Bake: 35 min | Oven: 350F | Serves: 8

Mandatory on every Thanksgiving card table since 1955! My secret upgrade swaps canned beans for blanched crisp-tender fresh green beans plus half a teaspoon of soy sauce for savory depth.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Fresh Trimmed Green Beans1.5 lbs (blanched 4 min)
Cream of Mushroom Soup1 can (10.75 oz)
Whole Milk & Soy Sauce1/2 cup milk + 1/2 tsp soy
French's Crispy Fried Onions1.5 cups (divided)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Blanch cut green beans in salted boiling water 4 minutes, then shock in ice water so they stay vibrant emerald green.
  2. Whisk soup, milk, soy sauce, and cracked black pepper in a 2-quart casserole; stir in beans and half the fried onions.
  3. Bake 25 minutes at 350F, scatter remaining fried onions on top, and bake 8 more minutes until golden brown.

Cook's Tip: Saute 4 oz sliced cremini mushrooms in butter and fold them in if feeding serious mushroom fans!
