Cinnamon Oatmeal Apple Crisp & Brown Betty

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Bake: 45 min | Oven: 350F | Serves: 8

Autumn orchard magic: tart Granny Smith apples hold their bite beneath a cinnamon-nutmeg old-fashioned oat topping baked until apple juices bubble thick at the edges.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Granny Smith & Braeburn Apples6 large (peeled, sliced 1/4-in)
Lemon Juice & Cinnamon1 Tbsp lemon + 1 tsp cinnamon
Old-Fashioned Rolled Oats1 cup
Flour & Dark Brown Sugar3/4 cup flour + 3/4 cup brown sugar
Cold Unsalted Butter1/2 cup (cubed)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Toss sliced apples with lemon juice, 2 Tbsp sugar, 1 Tbsp flour, and half the cinnamon; pack into a buttered 9-inch square pan.
  2. Rub cold butter cubes into oats, flour, brown sugar, pinch of salt, and nutmeg with fingertips until pea-sized clumps form.
  3. Scatter oat streusel evenly over apples and bake 45 min at 350F until juices syrup up around the corners.

Cook's Tip: Serve warm in cereal bowls with a big scoop of French vanilla bean ice cream melting into the oats.
