Slow-Braised Chuck Pot Roast with Root Vegetables

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 25 min | Braise: 3.5 hrs | Oven: 300F | Serves: 6

Low-and-slow cast-iron Dutch oven magic turns an inexpensive marbled chuck roast into spoon-tender Sunday supper with caramelized onions and sweet carrots.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Boneless Beef Chuck Roast3.5 lb (tied, patted dry)
Yellow Onions & Garlic2 large sliced + 4 cloves
Beef Broth & Tomato Paste2 cups broth + 2 Tbsp paste
Yukon Golds & Carrots1.5 lbs chunked + 4 carrots
Fresh Rosemary & Thyme2 sprigs each

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Salt and pepper chuck roast generously; sear in oil inside a heavy Dutch oven 5 min per side until deep brown crust forms.
  2. Remove beef, saute onions and tomato paste 5 min, deglaze browned bits with beef broth, and nestle roast back in halfway submerged.
  3. Cover tightly and braise at 300F for 2 hours; tuck potatoes and carrots around roast and braise 90 min longer until fork-tender.

Cook's Tip: Skim fat off braising liquid and simmer down with 1 Tbsp butter for glossy pan gravy!
