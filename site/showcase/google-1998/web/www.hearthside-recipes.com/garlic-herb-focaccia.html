Sheet-Pan Rosemary & Sea Salt Focaccia

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 25 min | Rise: 2 hrs | Bake: 425F | Yield: 1 half-sheet

Crispy olive-oil fried underside, pillowy bubbly crumb inside, and fragrant rosemary needles on top. Even beginners nail this sheet-pan focaccia on the first try!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
All-Purpose or Bread Flour4 cups
Instant Yeast & Salt2 tsp yeast + 2 tsp kosher salt
Warm Water (100F)1.75 cups
Extra-Virgin Olive Oil1/3 cup total (divided)
Fresh Rosemary & Flaky Salt2 Tbsp chopped + 1 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Stir flour, yeast, salt, water, and 2 Tbsp olive oil into a shaggy sticky dough; cover and let double 90 minutes.
  2. Pour 3 Tbsp olive oil into a rimmed half-sheet pan, turn dough out, stretch gently toward corners, and proof 35 min.
  3. Oil fingertips and press deep dimples all over dough; scatter rosemary and flaky salt, then bake 22-25 min at 425F.

Cook's Tip: Slice into thick squares and split horizontally for incredible heirloom tomato & mozzarella sandwiches!
