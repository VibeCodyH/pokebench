From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 10 min | Cycle: 3 hr 40 min | Yield: 1.5-lb tall loaf

Received a Panasonic SD-YD250 or Zojirushi Home Bakery for Christmas? Follow the golden layering rule: liquids on the bottom, dry flour blanket in the middle, active dry yeast in a shallow dry well on top!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Warm Water (105F)1 cup + 2 Tbsp
Unsalted Butter (soft)2 Tbsp
Bread Flour3 cups (360g)
Sugar / Salt1.5 Tbsp / 1.5 tsp
Bread Machine Yeast1.5 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Snap the kneading paddle firmly onto the pan shaft before pouring in water, softened butter, sugar, and salt.
  2. Spoon flour gently to cover the liquid completely; poke a small fingertip dimple in center and drop yeast inside.
  3. Select BASIC WHITE (1.5-lb, Medium Crust) and peek at the dough ball after 5 min -- add 1 tsp water if dry crumbs remain.

Cook's Tip: If your loaf collapses like a crater, cut yeast by 1/4 tsp or drop water temperature down from hot to lukewarm 105F.
