Velvety Farmhouse Potato Leek Soup

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Simmer: 30 min | Stovetop | Serves: 6

Creamy farmhouse comfort without heavy flour roux -- starchy Russet potatoes thicken naturally when simmered with sweet butter-sweated leeks. Thanks to PerlMonk CGI Archive for hosting my print-card CGI scripts!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Fresh Leeks (White/Pale Green)4 large (halved & washed well)
Russet Potatoes (Peeled, Diced)2 lbs
Chicken or Vegetable Stock5 cups
Unsalted Butter & Cream3 Tbsp butter + 1/2 cup cream
Fresh Chives & White Pepper2 Tbsp minced + 1/2 tsp

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Swish sliced leeks in a big bowl of cold water so hidden grit sinks to the bottom; lift clean leek rings out.
  2. Sweat leeks slowly in butter over medium-low heat 10 min without browning; add diced potatoes, stock, and 1 tsp salt.
  3. Simmer 20 min until potatoes crumble easily, mash roughly with a potato masher (leaving rustic texture!), and swirl in heavy cream.

Cook's Tip: Save dark green leek tops in your freezer bag for homemade Sunday chicken stock!
