Deep-Dish Cast Iron Peach Cobbler

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Bake: 40 min | Oven: 375F | Serves: 8

Southern self-rising magic: pour sweet milk batter directly on top of melted butter inside a cast-iron skillet, spoon sugared summer peaches over it, and never stir -- the cake rises right up around the fruit! Found via Bamboozle Portal!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Sliced Ripe Peaches5 cups (tossed w/ 1/2 cup sugar)
Unsalted Butter (Melted in Skillet)1/2 cup (1 stick)
Flour, Sugar & Baking Powder1 cup + 1 cup + 2 tsp
Whole Milk & Nutmeg1 cup milk + 1/4 tsp nutmeg

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Melt butter inside a 10-inch cast-iron skillet while preheating oven to 375F.
  2. Whisk flour, 1 cup sugar, baking powder, pinch salt, and milk into smooth thin batter; pour straight over melted butter (DO NOT STIR!).
  3. Spoon sugared peaches and juices gently over batter center, sprinkle nutmeg, and bake 40-45 min until golden puffed.

Cook's Tip: Works brilliantly with canned cling peaches drained of half their syrup during mid-winter cravings!
