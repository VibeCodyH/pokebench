Brown-Butter Bakery Chocolate Chip Cookies

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 20 min | Chill: 1 hr | Bake: 350F | Yield: 28 cookies

Why do bakery cookies taste richer and chewier than standard Toll House? Browning the butter in a saucepan adds nutty toffee aroma, and chilling the dough prevents flat puddles.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Unsalted Butter (Browned)1 cup (2 sticks)
Dark Brown Sugar & White Sugar1 cup + 1/2 cup
Eggs + 1 Extra Yolk1 whole + 1 yolk, 2 tsp vanilla
All-Purpose Flour & Soda2.25 cups + 1 tsp baking soda
Semisweet Chocolate Chips2 cups (12-oz bag)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Melt butter in a light-colored skillet over medium heat until foam subsides and toasted brown milk specks smell nutty; cool 15 min.
  2. Beat browned butter with sugars 2 min, beat in egg, extra yolk, and vanilla, then fold in flour, soda, 1 tsp salt, and chocolate chips.
  3. Chill dough 1 hour, scoop 2-Tbsp balls onto parchment sheets, and bake 10-11 min at 350F until edges brown while centers stay soft.

Cook's Tip: Pull cookies out while centers still look slightly underdone -- they firm up perfectly on the hot sheet pan.
