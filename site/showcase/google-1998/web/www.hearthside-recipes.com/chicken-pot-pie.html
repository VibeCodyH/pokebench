Flaky Double-Crust Chicken Pot Pie from Scratch

From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 40 min | Bake: 45 min | Oven: 400F | Serves: 6

Sunday company comfort food at its finest: tender roasted chicken thighs, carrots, and pearl onions tucked inside a double crust made with ice-cold butter and flaky pastry shortening.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Double Pie Crust Pastry2 chilled discs (all-butter)
Shredded Roast Chicken3 cups cooked thigh & breast
Carrots, Celery & Peas1 cup each diced
Chicken Stock & Heavy Cream2 cups stock + 1/2 cup cream
Butter, Flour & Fresh Thyme4 Tbsp butter + 1/3 cup flour

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Sweat diced carrots and celery in butter 6 min; sprinkle in flour and cook 2 min before whisking in hot stock and cream until thick.
  2. Fold in shredded chicken, frozen peas, thyme, and black pepper; let filling cool 15 min so bottom crust stays crisp.
  3. Line a 9-inch deep dish with bottom crust, fill, cap with top crust, crimp edges, cut 4 steam vents, and bake 45 min at 400F.

Cook's Tip: Place a foil-lined cookie sheet on the lower rack to catch bubbling gravy drips cleanly!
