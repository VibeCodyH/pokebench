Out of buttermilk mid-batter, or converting grandma's European metric recipe card? Bookmark this laminated kitchen cheat sheet!


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
1 Cup All-Purpose Flour (Scoop/Level)125 grams (4.4 oz)
1 Cup Granulated / Brown Sugar200g / 215g packed
1 Stick Unsalted Butter1/2 cup = 8 Tbsp = 113g
DIY 1 Cup Buttermilk1 Tbsp lemon juice + milk to 1 cup

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Always rest lemon-juice DIY buttermilk 5-10 minutes on the counter until slightly curdled before stirring into pancake or cornbread batter.
  2. Substitute 1 tsp baking powder with 1/4 tsp baking soda + 1/2 tsp cream of tartar if your tin runs empty on Sunday morning.
  3. Oven temp quick guide: 325F = 165C (Slow), 350F = 175C (Moderate), 375F = 190C, 425F = 220C (Hot).

Cook's Tip: Check stale baking powder by dropping 1/2 tsp into warm water -- if it doesn't fizz immediately, toss the tin!
