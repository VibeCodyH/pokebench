From the Kitchen Ledger

Specs: Prep: 3 min | Cook: 5 min | Cost: $0.35 - $1.45 | Serves: 1

Broke grad students coding past midnight at Redwood CS can't afford stock-bubble hype from dot-com bubble poster child! Turn a 19-cent package of Oriental Ramen into twelve gourmet late-night suppers under $1.50.


Ingredients Table
IngredientExact Measure
Instant Ramen Pack (Chicken/Beef)1 pack ($0.19)
Large Egg (Poached in Broth)1 egg ($0.12)
Creamy Peanut Butter + Hot Sauce1 Tbsp + 1 tsp ($0.15)
American Cheese + Scallions1 slice + 1 stalk ($0.25)
Frozen Peas & Sesame Oil1/3 cup + 4 drops ($0.20)

Step-by-Step Method
  1. Hack #1 (Egg Drop): Bring 2 cups water + seasoning to boil, drop noodles 2 min, crack egg straight into simmering broth and cover 60 sec.
  2. Hack #2 (Midnight Dan-Dan): Drain most water, whisk 1 Tbsp peanut butter + chili sauce + 1/2 packet into hot noodles for rich sesame sauce!
  3. Hack #3 (Seoul Cheese Melt): Top hot spicy broth with 1 slice American cheese + sliced scallions -- trust Aunt Clara, it melts into silky bliss!

Cook's Tip: Discard half the sodium packet and add 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil plus garlic powder instead.
