NeXTstep Style with WindowMaker 0.19 vs FVWM2
Borealis Linux 5.2 Documentation Project | Updated 11-Sep-1998

Choosing an X11 window manager on a 32MB laptop or 64MB desktop comes down to memory footprint vs eye candy. WindowMaker 0.19 delivers crisp NeXTstep dock tiles in under 5MB, whereas FVWM2 sips barely 2.1MB.
