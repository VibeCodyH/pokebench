XFree86 3.3.2 XF86Config & CRT Modeline Safety Guide
Borealis Linux 5.2 Documentation Project | Updated 11-Sep-1998
CRT HARDWARE WARNING: Driving an older fixed-frequency 14-inch or 15-inch SVGA monitor outside its horizontal scan range can overheat the flyback coil. Always verify HorizSync and VertRefresh in your monitor manual before starting XFree86 3.3.2!

Configure /etc/X11/XF86Config using xf86config or XF86Setup. For S3 ViRGE and ATI Mach64 cards, 16bpp avoids 24bpp framebuffer glitches.

Section "Monitor"
    Identifier  "Sony Trinitron 17seII"
    HorizSync   31.5 - 82.0
    VertRefresh 50.0 - 120.0
    Modeline "1024x768" 78.80 1024 1040 1136 1312 768 769 772 800 +hsync +vsync
EndSection
