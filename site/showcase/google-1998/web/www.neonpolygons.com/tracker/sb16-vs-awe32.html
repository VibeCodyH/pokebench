Liner Notes

Building a late-90s DOS/Win95 music box? Compare the trusty SoundBlaster 16 (OPL3 FM chip) against the SoundBlaster AWE32 with its 30-pin SIMM memory and Waveblaster header!


SB16 vs. AWE32 Hardware Matrix
SpecSoundBlaster 16 (CT2230)SoundBlaster AWE32 (CT3900)
Synth EngineYamaha YMF262 OPL3 (4-op FM)EMU8000 32-voice wavetable + OPL3
Sample RAMNone (ROM/FM only)Up to 28 MB via dual 30-pin SIMM slots (load custom SoundFonts!)
Waveblaster HeaderYes (accepts Roland SCD-15 / DB50XG)Yes (though EMU8000 already covers GM/GS nicely)
DOS ConfigSET BLASTER=A220 I5 D1 H5 P330 T6AWEUTIL /S for DOS General MIDI synth init
