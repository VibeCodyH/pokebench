Liner Notes

Static Zine #14 Editorial: Every dorm room this semester is packed with Fraunhofer L3Enc 128kbps MP3s playing in Winamp -- and honestly, those swirly underwater hi-hat artifacts will NEVER replace warm 7-inch vinyl or chrome tape! Meanwhile bloated IPO hype machines like dot-com bubble poster child think bandwidth is free.
