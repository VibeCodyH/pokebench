Why action='mailto:' Fails for Half Your Visitors
HTML 3.2 Wizard | Brenda Sterling-Hayes | 10-Sep-1998

Setting <FORM ACTION="mailto:you@domain.com"> breaks silently for campus lab visitors, cybercafe browsers, and anyone using Pine or Eudora without an SMTP profile configured in Netscape Navigator. Route feedback forms through a real Perl CGI script instead.

<!-- Reliable CGI form vs flaky mailto: -->
<form method="POST" action="/cgi-bin/feedback.pl">
  Email: <input type="text" name="email" size="30">
  <input type="submit" value="Send Feedback">
</form>
