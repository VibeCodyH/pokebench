Optimizing Animated GIF89a Banners Under 15 Kilobytes
HTML 3.2 Wizard | Brenda Sterling-Hayes | 10-Sep-1998

On a 28.8k or 56k dialup modem, every 10 kilobytes adds 2-3 seconds of wait time. Keep animated GIF89a banners under 15 KB by limiting global palettes to 16 colors, cropping sub-frames to changed rectangles, and avoiding 30-frame fades.
