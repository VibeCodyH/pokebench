How to Embed a WebRing Navigation Table cleanly on Your Page
HTML 3.2 Wizard | Brenda Sterling-Hayes | 10-Sep-1998

Joining a WebRing connects independent hobbyist sites cleanly, provided you drop their navigation bar inside a compact bordered <TABLE> with explicit cell widths instead of unstyled center blocks.

<table width="440" cellpadding="4" cellspacing="0" border="1" bgcolor="#eeeeee">
  <tr><td align="center"><font size="2">
    [Prev] | [List Sites] | [Random] | [Next]
  </font></td></tr>
</table>
