Why Framesets Break Bookmarks and Search Engines
HTML 3.2 Wizard | Brenda Sterling-Hayes | 10-Sep-1998

<FRAMESET> layouts look slick for five minutes until users realize clicking Bookmark saves only the outer frame URL, printing cuts off the navigation frame, and 1998 search spiders indexing <NOFRAMES> miss your internal links entirely.

<!-- BAD: crawlers see empty NOFRAMES unless you duplicate nav links -->
<frameset cols="180,*">
  <frame src="nav.html" name="sidebar">
  <frame src="welcome.html" name="main">
</frameset>
