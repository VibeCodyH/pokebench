Client-Side Image Maps (<MAP> & <AREA>) vs Server-Side Maps
HTML 3.2 Wizard | Brenda Sterling-Hayes | 10-Sep-1998

Client-side image maps (<MAP> + <AREA>) avoid a CGI server round-trip on every click, show real target URLs in Netscape's bottom status bar, and work when pages are saved locally to disk.

<img src="campus_map.gif" usemap="#campus" border="0" width="320" height="180">
<map name="campus">
  <area shape="rect" coords="10,10,140,80" href="lab.html" alt="Soda Hall">
</map>
