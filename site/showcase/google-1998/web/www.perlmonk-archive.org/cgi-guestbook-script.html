Writing a Flat-File Perl 5 CGI Guestbook with flock()
PerlMonk 5.004 Archive | Randall Mercer & Contributors | 09-Sep-1998

Flat-file CGI guestbooks routinely lose entries or zero out their data file when two Netscape visitors click Submit simultaneously. Always acquire an exclusive advisory lock with flock(FH, 2) and escape HTML angle brackets before appending.

#!/usr/bin/perl -wT
use strict;
use CGI qw(:standard);
use Fcntl qw(:flock);
my $name = escapeHTML(param('name') || 'Anon');
open(my $fh, '>>', '/home/httpd/data/guestbook.txt') or die $!;
flock($fh, LOCK_EX) or die $!;
print $fh "$name\t" . scalar(localtime) . "\n";
close($fh);

Production Note: Never skip flock() on shared campus NFS or Apache prefork servers.

Perl 5.004 Environment & Testing Note: Verify scripts from the shell with perl -wcT script.cgi before copying them into /usr/local/apache/cgi-bin. Under Apache mod_perl or standard CGI, inspect /var/log/httpd/error_log with tail -f to catch premature end-of-script-headers and uninitialized $_ warnings.
