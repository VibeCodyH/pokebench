Persistent Inverted Indexes with DB_File & tie() in Perl 5
PerlMonk 5.004 Archive | Randall Mercer & Contributors | 09-Sep-1998

Avoid loading multi-megabyte inverted word indexes into Perl hashes on every CGI search hit. Use DB_File with tie() to query Berkeley DB B-tree posting lists directly from disk in constant memory.

#!/usr/bin/perl -w
use strict;
use DB_File;
tie my %inv, 'DB_File', '/var/db/crawl_inv.db', O_RDONLY, 0644, $DB_BTREE or die $!;
my $postings = $inv{'pagerank'} || '';
untie %inv;

Production Note: Pack document IDs and TF weights with pack('N*', ...) to shrink index files 60%.

Perl 5.004 Environment & Testing Note: Verify scripts from the shell with perl -wcT script.cgi before copying them into /usr/local/apache/cgi-bin. Under Apache mod_perl or standard CGI, inspect /var/log/httpd/error_log with tail -f to catch premature end-of-script-headers and uninitialized $_ warnings.
