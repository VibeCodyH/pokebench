Nightly FTP Mirror Scripts with Net::FTP and Cron
PerlMonk 5.004 Archive | Randall Mercer & Contributors | 09-Sep-1998

Mirror Borealis Linux RPM trees or RFC directories automatically at 3:15 AM using Net::FTP and crontab -e. Always call $ftp->binary() before fetching tarballs or ISO files, otherwise ASCII CR/LF conversion corrupts archives.

use Net::FTP;
my $ftp = Net::FTP->new('ftp.cs.redwood.edu', Passive => 1) or die $@;
$ftp->login('anonymous', 'mirror@perlmonk-archive.org');
$ftp->binary();
$ftp->get('/mirrors/borealis-5.2/MD5SUMS');

Production Note: Check exit status and mail errors to root so stale mirrors don't go unnoticed.

Perl 5.004 Environment & Testing Note: Verify scripts from the shell with perl -wcT script.cgi before copying them into /usr/local/apache/cgi-bin. Under Apache mod_perl or standard CGI, inspect /var/log/httpd/error_log with tail -f to catch premature end-of-script-headers and uninitialized $_ warnings.
