Fan Fiction Transmission

Red alert strobes painted the bridge bulkheads in rhythmic crimson pulses as the U.S.S. Horizon shuddered against the outer shoals of the Thorn Nebula. "Deflectors down to fourteen percent, Captain!" shouted Lieutenant Sato over the whine of overloaded stabilizers.

Captain Vance-Mercer gripped the brass rail behind helm. Across the pit, Commander T'Kar did not flinch as sparks rained from an overhead optical conduit. "The Vorlak cruiser is masking its warp signature inside the ionized dust band," T'Kar rumbled. "If they fire disruptors at point-blank range, our hull plating will buckle in six seconds."

"Then we don't give them six seconds," Mercer replied softly. "Helm, cut impulse, vent port plasma manifolds on my mark, and ignite it with a low-yield phaser burst. Let's light up this fog."
