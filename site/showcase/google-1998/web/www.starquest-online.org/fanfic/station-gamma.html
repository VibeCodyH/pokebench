Subspace Briefing

Lower-Decks Vignette: Ever wonder what happens at 03:00 station time while the command crew is asleep? Join Chief Engineer Stanley Kowalski in Sublevel 4.


Fan Fiction Transmission

Nothing in the Starfleet Technical Manual mentions what to do when a Vorlak merchant frigate docks at Bay 3 with a leaking thermal manifold and zero spare gaskets. Chief Kowalski sipped lukewarm synthetic French roast from a chipped mug and reached for his roll of military-grade silvery hull tape.

"Thirty-two billion credits of subspace telemetry," Kowalski muttered to the junior tech beside him, "and the entire sector relay still runs on a flaky optical coupler that drops packets whenever someone heats up chili in the mess hall microwave." A sharp chime echoed across the bulkhead: Docking clamps pressurized. Crisis averted until morning shift.
