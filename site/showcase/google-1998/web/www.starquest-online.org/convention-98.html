Subspace Briefing

Just got back from Redwood Con '98 held Saturday, August 29 at the Redwood Ramada Inn Ballroom! Over 400 fans packed the main hall for the StarQuest panel, dealer room VHS swaps, and homemade phaser prop showcase.
