Subspace Briefing

Season 2 (1997-1998) ramped up the Obsidian Reach border war and introduced Station Gamma-9 as a recurring forward base. Tape swap alerts: if anyone has a clean broadcast copy of #208 without local weather crawl, ICQ me!


Season 2 Episode Log (1997-1998)
Ep#Title & Guest CastAir DateKey Plot & Nielsen Notes
#201Siege of Gamma-9
(Guest: J. Morales as Cmdr. Diaz)		17-Sep-97Three Vorlak destroyers test the station's new tri-cobalt shields; Horizon arrives at Warp 9.6 with phasers hot.
#202A Matter of Phase Variance
(Guest: T. Bell as Lt. Vance)		24-Sep-97Subspace phase-cloak prototype phases half of Deck 4 out of normal baryonic space. Huge fan debate on Usenet.
#203T'Kar's Oath
(Guest: R. Briggs as Warlord Vorn)		15-Oct-97T'Kar confronts his estranged brother over stolen Vorlak disruptor schematics. Rated 4.2 syndicated household share.
#204Quantum Drift
(Guest: S. Takahashi as Dr. Lin)		12-Nov-97Horizon slips into a mirror sector where the Valiant Class never escaped the Thorn Nebula.
#205Betrayal at Obsidian Reach (Pt 1 & 2)
(Season Finale)		20-May-98Cliffhanger! Admiral Thorne orders Horizon into forbidden space as tachyon mines detonate around the nacelle pylons.
