Subspace Briefing

Personnel dossier on Captain Elena Vance-Mercer (portrayed by Joanna Reyes), commanding officer of the U.S.S. Horizon. Known for unorthodox gravitational slingshot maneuvers and refusing Admiralty stand-down orders during the Gamma-9 crisis. Her cybernetic tactical simulator lectures were even debated at Redwood State University CS Dept.
