Redwood CS Graduate Student Handbook (1998-1999)
Graduate Admissions & Advising Committee

Welcome to the Redwood State University Computer Science Ph.D. and M.S. programs. Full-time Teaching and Research Assistants receive a 9-month stipend of $13,800 plus full tuition remission, a shared desk in Soda Hall 4th-floor bullpens, and an account on turing.cs.redwood.edu with 150MB NFS disk quota.

  • Breadth Requirement: Pass 3 core area courses (Systems, Theory, AI/IR) by Year 2.
  • Qualifying Exam: Oral research proposal defense before a 3-faculty committee.
  • Lab Keys: See Room 405 coordinator with your student ID and Unix account form.

Soda Hall Support & Quick Checklist: Before emailing helpdesk@cs.redwood.edu, verify disk blocks with quota -v, confirm your NFS automount path under /home/grad or /usr/local/bin, and record the SPARCstation hostname printed on the bezel. Paper jams in Room 310 belong in the log binder beside the HP LaserJet 4M rather than restarting the lpd spooler.
