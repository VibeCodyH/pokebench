CS 312: Operating Systems Internals on SPARC & Linux
Prof. Elena Rostova | Soda Hall 306, MWF 1:00 PM | Fall 1998

Kernel trap handling, preemptive scheduling, POSIX threads, spinlocks vs semaphores, virtual memory page tables, SPARC TLB miss handlers, and ext2/UFS filesystem internals.

Required Text: Silberschatz & Galvin, Operating System Concepts (5th ed.)
Grading: Programming Projects 50% | Midterm 20% | Final Exam 25% | Lab Participation 5%

ScheduleTopics Covered
Weeks 1-3System calls, kernel stacks, context switching
Weeks 4-7Mutexes, condition variables, deadlock prevention
Weeks 8-11Paging, two-level page tables, clock replacement
Weeks 12-14Disk scheduling, inodes, ext2fs & NFS RPC
