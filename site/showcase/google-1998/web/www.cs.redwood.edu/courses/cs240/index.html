CS 240: Data Structures, Hash Tables & Graph Traversal
Prof. Beatrice Calloway | Soda Hall 306, TuTh 11:00 AM | Fall 1998

Asymptotic complexity, open-addressing and chained hash tables, AVL/red-black balanced trees, priority queues, BFS/DFS, Dijkstra shortest paths, and directed hyperlink graph analysis.

Required Text: Cormen, Leiserson & Rivest, Introduction to Algorithms (CLR)
Grading: Programming Projects 50% | Midterm 20% | Final Exam 25% | Lab Participation 5%

ScheduleTopics Covered
Weeks 1-3Big-O proofs, dynamic arrays, stacks, queues
Weeks 4-7Hash tables, universal hashing, balanced BSTs
Weeks 8-11Heaps, union-find, BFS/DFS, topological sort
Weeks 12-14Dijkstra, MSTs, strongly connected components
