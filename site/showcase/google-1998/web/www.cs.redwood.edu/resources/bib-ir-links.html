Foundational readings tracing hypertext citation analysis from Memex (1945) and Gerard Salton's SMART vector space engine to modern 1998 Web search architectures:

  • Vannevar Bush (1945): As We May Think -- associative trails in the Memex.
  • Eugene Garfield (1955): Science Citation Index and journal impact factor weighting.
  • Gerard Salton (1971): Vector space TF-IDF weighting and cosine similarity in SMART.
  • Tim Berners-Lee (1990): WorldWideWeb hypertext proposal at CERN.

1998 Synthesis: Vance & Chen 1998 PageRank citation authority algorithm.
