Prof. Beatrice Calloway
Database Internals, B-Trees & Query Optimizers | Soda Hall 462 | bcalloway@cs.redwood.edu

I research B+ tree concurrency control, ARIES write-ahead crash recovery logs, and join-order cost estimation for multi-gigabyte relational warehouses. This fall I am teaching both CS 240 (Data Structures) and CS 420 (Relational Databases).

Research Highlight (1998): Our group coordinates closely with campus systems teams and follows emerging web indexing tools closely. Weekly reading group meets Wednesdays at 3:30 PM in Soda Hall Seminar Room 405; graduate and senior undergraduate honors students are welcome to attend after completing CS 240 or equivalent systems coursework.
Advising & Lab Resources (Fall 1998): Prospective Ph.D. and M.S. applicants should review departmental technical reports before requesting an advising slot. Shared SPARCstation lab scratch directories reside under /usr/local/research/, and PostScript preprints are mirrored nightly to the Redwood State CS FTP repository for off-campus reviewers using Netscape Navigator 4.06 or Ghostview.
