PostScript Printing, lpr Queues & Quota Policy
Sysadmin Greg Lindqvist | Soda Hall Printing Services

Each CS student receives a 250-page semester laser printing quota across our three HP LaserJet 4M Plus duplex PostScript printers (hp4m-2f, hp4m-3f, and hp4m-4f). Always use enscript -2r for two-up landscape source listings and check queue status before re-submitting stuck PostScript jobs.

% enscript -2r -Php4m-3f source.c   # 2-up landscape saves quota
% lpq -Php4m-3f                     # check print queue
% lprm -Php4m-3f 418                # cancel runaway job 418

Prohibited: Printing 300-page IETF RFCs or raw binary files will exhaust your quota and trigger an automatic banner-page hold.


Soda Hall Support & Quick Checklist: Before emailing helpdesk@cs.redwood.edu, verify disk blocks with quota -v, confirm your NFS automount path under /home/grad or /usr/local/bin, and record the SPARCstation hostname printed on the bezel. Paper jams in Room 310 belong in the log binder beside the HP LaserJet 4M rather than restarting the lpd spooler.
