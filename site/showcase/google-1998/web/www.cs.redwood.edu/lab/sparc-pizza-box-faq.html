Redwood CS Lab FAQ: SunOS 4.1.4 & SPARCstation 5 Guide
Maintained by Sysadmin Greg Lindqvist (Soda Hall 260)

The 3rd-floor instructional labs run 48 Sun SPARCstation 5 "pizza-box" workstations (85MHz microSPARC-II, 64MB RAM, 17-inch Trinitron CRT) running Solaris 2.6, plus four legacy SunOS 4.1.4 SparcClassic boxes for backward binary compatibility. Home directories are mounted over NFS from turing:/export/home.

# Useful Soda Hall SPARC commands:
% rup                  # check lab machine uptime and load
% ypcat passwd | grep $USER
% xhost +localhost     # DO NOT run 'xhost +' globally!

Emergency Notice: Never press Stop-A unless the Xsun server is completely wedged; dropping to the ok> OpenBoot PROM halts background NFS jobs.


Soda Hall Support & Quick Checklist: Before emailing helpdesk@cs.redwood.edu, verify disk blocks with quota -v, confirm your NFS automount path under /home/grad or /usr/local/bin, and record the SPARCstation hostname printed on the bezel. Paper jams in Room 310 belong in the log binder beside the HP LaserJet 4M rather than restarting the lpd spooler.
