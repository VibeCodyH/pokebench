Configuring Pine, Elm, and .forward Files on Turing
Redwood CS Lab Staff | Mail Server mail.cs.redwood.edu

Campus mail arrives via sendmail 8.9.1 on turing.cs.redwood.edu. Students may read mail directly on Unix via Pine 4.02 or Elm 2.4, or configure POP3 in Netscape Messenger 4.06. To filter mailing lists into separate mbox folders, set up a .forward pipeline invoking procmail.

# ~/.forward (chmod 644 ~/.forward)
"|IFS=' ' && exec /usr/local/bin/procmail -f- || exit 75 #username"

# ~/.procmailrc snippet for borealis-dev list
:0:
* ^Sender:.*borealis-dev@lists.penguinos.org
Mail/borealis-dev
