NOTICE: Quake II & Doom Deathmatch Restrictions in Soda Hall Lab
Sysadmin Greg Lindqvist | Effective Immediately (Fall 1998)

Our 10Base-T instructional lab subnet saturated completely Tuesday afternoon when twelve terminals ran IPX Doom and UDP Quake II deathmatches simultaneously during CS 312 lab hours. NFS home directory mounts timed out across the entire third floor.

Lab Policy: Networked Quake II, Doom, and Unreal sessions are strictly prohibited between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM Monday-Friday. Violators will have quake2 processes terminated with kill -9 and lab privileges suspended for one week.

Soda Hall Support & Quick Checklist: Before emailing helpdesk@cs.redwood.edu, verify disk blocks with quota -v, confirm your NFS automount path under /home/grad or /usr/local/bin, and record the SPARCstation hostname printed on the bezel. Paper jams in Room 310 belong in the log binder beside the HP LaserJet 4M rather than restarting the lpd spooler.
