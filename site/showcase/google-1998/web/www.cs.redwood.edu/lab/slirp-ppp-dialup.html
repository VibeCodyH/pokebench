Redwood Campus 56k Modem Pool & PPP Dialup Instructions
Redwood Computing Center | Dialup Access Desk

Off-campus faculty and grad students can dial into our Ascend MAX 4048 rack supporting V.90 and K56flex 56k modems at (555) 019-2800. Idle connections drop after 20 minutes; maximum session length is 4 hours during evening peak hours (7 PM - 11 PM).

# /etc/ppp/options.ttyS1 for Linux/Solaris PPP clients
lock
crtscts
115200
noipdefault
defaultroute
mtu 576

If your USRobotics Sportster drops carrier during heavy downloads, set your terminal MTU to 576 and confirm AT&F1 hardware flow control.
