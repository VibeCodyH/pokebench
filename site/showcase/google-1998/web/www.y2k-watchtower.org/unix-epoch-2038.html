Why Unix/Linux Sysadmins Laugh at 2000 and Worry About 2038

While mainframe teams sweat YYMMDD columns, POSIX operating systems represent timestamps as signed 32-bit integers counting seconds since 00:00:00 UTC Jan 1, 1970. Unix sails smoothly past January 1, 2000 (time_t = 946684800), then overflows on January 19, 2038 at 03:14:07 UTC (2^31 - 1)!
