Y2K Glossary: Windowing, Date Expansion, Epoch & Century Byte

TermTechnical Definition (1998)
WindowingInterpreting 2-digit years against a pivot year (e.g., 00-49 -> 2000-2049).
Date ExpansionWidening database columns and copybooks from YYMMDD to YYYYMMDD.
CMOS Century ByteRTC register 0x32 storing BCD century (0x19 vs 0x20).
EpochZero-reference timestamp (Unix: Jan 1, 1970; DOS: Jan 1, 1980).
