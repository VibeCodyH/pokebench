NERC Regional Power Grid Y2K Compliance Progress Report (Q3 1998)

The North American Electric Reliability Council (NERC) filed its September 1998 readiness assessment with the U.S. Department of Energy. Generation turbines themselves run on analog governors and cycle clocks, whereas energy management systems (EMS), billing meters, and substation RTUs require patching:

Grid LayerQ3 1998 AuditedTarget Remediation
Bulk Generation Plants78% inventoriedJune 30, 1999
Transmission EMS / SCADA64% remediatedJune 30, 1999
Distribution Substations59% testedSeptember 1999

NERC expects localized billing hiccups rather than nationwide grid collapse, provided utilities hit June 1999 milestones.
