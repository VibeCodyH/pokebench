Embedded Microcontrollers: Separating Elevator & Traffic Myths from Reality

Newsweeklies claim every elevator will plunge and every stoplight will freeze at midnight. In reality, over 97% of embedded chips count elapsed clock cycles or intervals rather than calendar dates. Risk concentrates in Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) tied to Windows NT/DOS SCADA schedulers:

System TypeCalendar Aware?Actual Risk
Traction ElevatorsRarely (service logs only)LOW -- safety brakes are mechanical
Traffic Signal ControllersYes (day-of-week plans)MEDIUM -- wrong weekday schedule
