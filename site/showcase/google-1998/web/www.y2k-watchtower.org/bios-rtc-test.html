How to Test Your PC BIOS & Real-Time Clock (1999 -> 2000 Rollover)

Many 1994-1997 Pentium and 486 motherboards store only two year digits in battery-backed CMOS RTC RAM and fail to increment the century byte (0x32) across midnight Dec 31, 1999. NEVER test inside Windows 95/98 -- expired trialware and file timestamps could break! Boot from a clean write-protected MS-DOS 6.22 floppy:

A:\> DATE 12-31-1999
A:\> TIME 23:58:00
--- Power PC completely OFF for 3 minutes! ---
--- Power ON from clean MS-DOS floppy and run: ---
A:\> DATE
Current date is Sat 01-01-2000   <-- PASS!
Current date is Tue 01-04-1980   <-- FAIL! Needs BIOS flash or TSR patch.
