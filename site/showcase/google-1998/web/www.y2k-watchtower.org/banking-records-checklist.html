Paper Trail Checklist: Printing Bank, Mortgage & 401k Statements

Even if your bank's IBM mainframe passes COBOL remediation cleanly, batch reconciliation glitches during January 2000 could delay corrected statements by weeks. Maintain physical paper proof:

  • Checking & Savings: Save every monthly statement from Jan 1999 through Feb 2000 in a fireproof folder.
  • Mortgage Amortization: Print current principal balance and escrow ledger before December 15, 1999.
  • 401(k) & Brokerage: Print quarterly share holdings (including cost basis!) and Social Security earnings printout.
  • Tax Returns: Keep paper copies of 1996-1998 Form 1040 filings plus W-2 slips.
