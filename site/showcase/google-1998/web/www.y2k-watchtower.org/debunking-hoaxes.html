Hoax Watch: No, Your Microwave Oven & Digital Toaster Will Not Explode

Tabloids and chain emails warn that kitchen blenders, VCRs, and coffee makers will seize up at 12:01 AM on January 1, 2000. Relax! Your GE microwave only tracks a 24-hour clock (12:00), and your VCR only cares about day-of-week for timer recordings (at worst, pick an identical calendar year like 1972!). Stop panic-buying impulse gadgets from even the dot-com bubble poster child and focus instead on real PC BIOS and banking records!

  • Toasters / Irons / Lamps: Purely thermal/mechanical -- zero microprocessors!
  • 1998 Automobiles: Engine ECUs count crankshaft revolutions and runtime hours, not years.
