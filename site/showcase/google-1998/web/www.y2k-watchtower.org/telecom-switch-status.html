Long-Distance Switches, PBX Systems & Emergency 911 Readiness

AT&T, MCI, and Sprint report that core Lucent 5ESS and Nortel DMS-100 central office switches already pass simulated 2000 billing rollovers. The weaker link sits in private office PBXs, voicemail servers, and older municipal E-911 call-logging terminals:

  • Toll-Billing CDR Logs: Older PBXs truncate 2000 call detail records unless EPROM firmware is updated.
  • 911 CAD Terminals: Many county dispatch centers run legacy OS/2 or DOS dispatch consoles under audit.
  • Local Resilience: Organize neighborhood backups -- see Neighborhood Block Captain Emergency Contact Tree Template.
