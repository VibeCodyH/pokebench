Two-Digit Year Traps in Excel 97, Lotus 1-2-3 & Access Databases

Office spreadsheets silently decide which century a typed 01/05/00 belongs to using a sliding pivot rule! Worse, legacy Lotus 1-2-3 and Excel 97 intentionally treat 1900 as a leap year (preserving a 1983 Lotus bug for backward compatibility, meaning serial date 60 is the non-existent Feb 29, 1900!).

  • Excel 95 / 97 Pivot Rule: Years 00-29 become 2000-2029; 30-99 become 1930-1999.
  • Lotus 1-2-3 Release 5: Typing 1/1/00 defaults to 1900 unless formatted with 4-digit year masks!
  • Remedy: Format every cell MM/DD/YYYY and re-enter 30-year bond maturities explicitly.
