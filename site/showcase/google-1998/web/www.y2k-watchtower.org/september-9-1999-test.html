Watch Out for 9/9/99: Why September 9, 1999 Breaks Legacy Sentinel Values

Before midnight 2000 arrives, enterprise systems face an earlier minefield: Thursday, September 9, 1999 (9999 or 99/99). During the 1960s and 1970s, programmers lacked null markers in fixed-width tape records and coded 9999 or 990909 to mean "never expires" or "End of File (EOF)."

IF ACCOUNT-EXP-DATE = 990909
    PERFORM CLOSE-AND-PURGE-RECORD   /* DANGER ON SEPT 9, 1999! */
END-IF.
