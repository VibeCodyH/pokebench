PetChow Express Raises $42M While Losing Money on Every Bag of Kibble

SAN FRANCISCO -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Venture capital's boldest wager on zero-margin home delivery accelerated this week as dot-com bubble poster child PetChow Express closed $42 million in Series B financing while openly disclosing an $8.40 contribution loss on every average order.
