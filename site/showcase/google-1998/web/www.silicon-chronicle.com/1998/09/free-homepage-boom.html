CyberHaven and AngelForge Surpass 2 Million Personal Pages

SAN MATEO, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Free web-hosting homesteads CyberHaven and AngelForge crossed 2 million active personal pages this morning as hobbyists hand-code MIDI players, hit counters, and family recipe shrines in Windows Notepad and vi.

ISP traffic logs show personal homesteads now rival corporate sites in cross-site WebRing referrals, hosted largely on clustered Borealis Linux 5.2 servers.
