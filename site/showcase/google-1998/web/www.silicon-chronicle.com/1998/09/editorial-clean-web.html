Editorial: Please Keep Pages Under 50KB for 28.8k Dialup Readers

EDITORIAL -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Too many web designers sitting on university T1 lines forget that 70% of home readers still connect over 28.8k or V.90 56k modems paying hourly ISP metered minutes. When even the dot-com bubble poster child takes 19 seconds to render bloated banner frames, frustrated readers hit the Netscape Stop button!

  • Optimize GIFs to 16 or 32 indexed colors; specify explicit width and height attributes.
  • Keep total HTML + image payload under 45 KB -- lean sites like The Y2K Watchtower prove speed wins loyalty.
