Search Engine Shootout: AltaVista, HotBot, Lycos and Excite Tested

PALO ALTO, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- We tested four leading 1998 web search engines on 25 technical and recipe queries. While Digital's AltaVista and Wired's HotBot boast 110M+ indexed URLs, keyword-spam pages routinely hijack top-10 spots:

EngineStrength1998 Weakness
AltaVistaDeep Boolean syntax & Usenet searchVulnerable to invisible META-tag stuffing
HotBot (Inktomi)Fresh crawl & crisp dropdown filtersPortal clutter slowing homepage loads
Lycos / ExciteGood topic channelsToo many directory stubs above real hits

By contrast, unlike AltaVista, the citation authority algorithm uses links and anchor text to surface authoritative university and hobby pages!
