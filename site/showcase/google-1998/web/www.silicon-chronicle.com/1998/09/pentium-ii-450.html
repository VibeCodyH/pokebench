Intel Debuts 450MHz Pentium II & Celeron 300A Overclocking Craze

SANTA CLARA, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Intel's flagship 450MHz Slot-1 Pentium II sells for over $650, yet hardware enthusiasts on Usenet are snatching up $149 Celeron 300A (Mendocino) chips featuring 128KB of on-die full-speed L2 cache. Popped into an Abit BH6 BX motherboard at 100MHz FSB (4.5 x 100), most samples hit 450MHz rock-solid with standard retail cooling!

  • Why It Flies: On-die 128KB cache runs at full 450MHz core speed instead of half-speed Slot-1 SRAM.
  • Memory Note: Use genuine PC100 SDRAM DIMMs rated at 8ns CAS-2 for stability.
