Bulk Beans & Generators Sell Out Ahead of 1999 Preparedness Wave

PORTLAND, Ore. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Pacific Northwest hardware co-ops and grain wholesalers report 4-week backorders on 50-lb pinto bean sacks, 55-gallon blue water drums, and Honda portable generators as suburban households quietly hedge against winter 1999 disruptions.

Rational preparedness guides like The Y2K Watchtower and Redwood University Y2K Software & Campus Clock Audit Task Force advise starting with simple home clock checks (How to Test Your PC BIOS & Real-Time Clock for the 1999->2000 Rollover) and 14 days of pantry rotation before hoarding extreme gear.
