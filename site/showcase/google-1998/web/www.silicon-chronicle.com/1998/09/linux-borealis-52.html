Borealis Linux 5.2 Gains Foothold in University Labs & ISPs

REDWOOD CITY, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Dialup internet service providers and computer science departments are increasingly replacing costly proprietary Unix seats with Borealis Linux 5.2, citing rock-solid Apache 1.3 performance and multi-CPU scaling on commodity dual Pentium II boxes.

  • Kernel Stability: Sysadmins praise 2.0.35 uptime and track 2.1 development in Linux 2.1 SMP Kernel Spinlocks & Dual Pentium II FAQ.
  • Cost Savings: A $2,400 dual-PII server running Borealis matches $18,000 RISC workstations for CGI workloads.
