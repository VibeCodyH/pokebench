Dorm Room Networks Flooded as Winamp and MP3 Trading Surge

CAMBRIDGE, Mass. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Returning college students armed with Nullsoft's skin-swapping Winamp 1.92 player and Fraunhofer 128 kbps MP3 encoders are saturating campus 10Base-T Ethernet and T1 external pipes by trading 4.2MB digital song files over IRC XDCC bots and Hotline servers.

Network administrators report 60% of night-time campus bandwidth now consists of MP3s rather than HTTP or MIDI downloads (compare lightweight tracker collections at GM/GS MIDI Sequence & Tracker Music WebRing).
