2x DVD-ROM Upgrade Kits: Worth $350 or Wait Until Next Year?

SAN JOSE, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Creative Labs' PC-DVD Encore Dxr2 kit ($349 with PCI MPEG-2 hardware decoder card) brings razor-sharp 4.7GB optical discs to Pentium II desktops -- yet only a handful of movies and encylopedia discs ship on DVD-ROM so far.

Most buyers are waiting for 4x/5x drives to drop below $199 while sticking with 24x CD-ROMs and Iomega 100MB Zip drives for everyday backups.
