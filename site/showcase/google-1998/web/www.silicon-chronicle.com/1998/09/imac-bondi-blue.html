Apple Drops Floppy Drive on Bondi Blue iMac: Bold Move or Madness?

CUPERTINO, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Apple's translucent teal $1,299 Bondi Blue iMac is flying off CompUSA shelves with its 233MHz PowerPC G3, built-in 56k modem, and swooping all-in-one CRT -- yet PC pundits remain stunned that Steve Jobs omitted the 3.5-inch floppy disk drive in favor of USB ports!

Included233MHz G3, 32MB RAM, 4GB EIDE HD, 24x CD-ROM, 2x USB, 10/100 Ethernet
MissingNo 1.44MB floppy, no ADB, no SCSI port ($129 Imation SuperDisk USB add-on)
