Uh-Oh! ICQ Instant Messenger Crosses 15 Million Registered UINs

TEL AVIV / NEW YORK -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- That unmistakable foghorn and "Uh-oh!" chime now echoes out of SoundBlaster 16 speakers worldwide as Mirabilis's ICQ ("I Seek You") passes 15 million registered UIN accounts following AOL's $287M summer acquisition.

Gamers coordinate Quake II and ChronoBlade 64 Sanctuary sessions via 8-digit UINs while office workers chat across firewalls.
