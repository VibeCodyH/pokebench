Why Perl 5 Remains the Duct Tape of 1998 E-Commerce

REDWOOD CITY, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Behind nearly every glossy storefront shipping books, CDs, or 50-lb pet food runs a fleet of Apache 1.3 CGI scripts written in Perl 5.004. Why? Because Larry Wall's text-processing Swiss Army knife parses form input, locks flat files with flock(), and talks to Oracle via DBI faster than compiled C++.

Explore production patterns at PerlMonk 5.004 CGI & Text Processing Archive and Writing a Flat-File Perl 5 CGI Guestbook with flock().
