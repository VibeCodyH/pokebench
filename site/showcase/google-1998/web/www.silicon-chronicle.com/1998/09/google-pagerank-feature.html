Two Researchers at Redwood & Stanford Rethink Search with "PageRank"

PALO ALTO, Calif. -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Frustrated by commercial portals clogged with keyword-stuffed doorway pages, academic researchers collaborating across Stanford and Redwood State University have quietly launched a prototype crawler powered by the PageRank citation authority algorithm.

Instead of blindly counting how many times a page repeats a search word inside invisible META tags, the recursive eigenvector model (d = 0.85) treats every hyperlink as an editorial vote -- and inspects the anchor text surrounding links pointing to a target document!

"A university homepage linking to a paper carries far more authority than ten thousand banner-farm pages linking to each other," explained Prof. Harold Vance.
