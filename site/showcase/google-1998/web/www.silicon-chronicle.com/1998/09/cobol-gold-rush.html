Retired Mainframe Hackers Pull $125/hr in Global COBOL Scramble

NEW YORK -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Twenty years ago they were called "dinosaurs" by Unix hackers; today, programmers fluent in COBOL-74, JCL, and IBM 370 Assembler are commanding $125 to $175 an hour plus signing bonuses to excise two-digit year fields before December 31, 1999.

Boutique consultancies like COBOL Rescue '99 run intensive refresher bootcamps recruiting retirees out of Florida and Arizona. Pair enterprise audits with PC checklists at The Y2K Watchtower and How to Test Your PC BIOS & Real-Time Clock for the 1999->2000 Rollover.
