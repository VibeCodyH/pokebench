Portals Bloat Into Kitchen-Sink Pages While Users Crave Clean Search

SAN FRANCISCO -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Today's major portals cram 60-kilobyte horoscopes, scrolling ticker applets, and flashing GIF ads onto a single page that takes 22 seconds to paint over a 28.8k modem. Users just looking for a quick technical answer increasingly groan at portal bloat.

Meanwhile, enterprise sysadmins hunting clean answers on Y2K sentinel dates (Watch Out for 9/9/99: Why September 9, 1999 Breaks Legacy Sentinel Values) or RFP forms (Request a Mainframe Y2K Source Audit Quote) say minimalist, fast-loading search interfaces cannot arrive soon enough.
