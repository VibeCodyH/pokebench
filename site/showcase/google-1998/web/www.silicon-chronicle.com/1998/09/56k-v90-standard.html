ITU Finalizes V.90 56k Modem Standard: ISPs Race to Upgrade Modems

GENEVA -- Sept. 14, 1998 -- Ending a bitter 18-month format war between USRobotics x2 and Lucent/Rockwell K56flex, the ITU formally ratified the unified V.90 56k modem specification. Regional dialup ISPs are rushing flash-ROM upgrades into Ascend MAX and 3Com Total Control racks this month.

  • FCC 53k Cap: Analog phone voltage rules limit real downstream connects to 48,000 - 52,000 bps.
  • Flash Caution: Check USRobotics Sportster serial numbers before flashing; some 1996 boards need hardware swap!
