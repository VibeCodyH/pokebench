Webvana Entrepreneur Pitch Guidelines (No Business Plan Over 3 Slides)

In 1998, writing a 40-page Microsoft Word business plan means a competitor in Austin already registered your domain name. Webvana partners review new startup pitches exclusively in 3-slide PowerPoint 97 format:

  1. Slide 1 -- The .com Name & TAM: State the domain (must end in .com) and claim a $50B+ offline market.
  2. Slide 2 -- The Hockey-Stick Chart: Show user growth hitting 5 million registered accounts by Q4 1999.
  3. Slide 3 -- The Burn & IPO Exit: Request $5M seed, project 18-month runway to Goldman Sachs roadshow.

No discounted cash flow spreadsheets! Leave room for Q&A over espresso.
