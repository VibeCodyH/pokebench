The Webvana Manifesto: Why Physical Bricks Are Obsolete by 2001

For five thousand years, commerce forced humans to walk into buildings made of concrete and bricks. With 28.8k and new 56k V.90 dialup modems entering 35 million American living rooms, concrete retail is dead weight.

"Steel shelves do not scale. Parking lots do not generate pageviews. Only a zero-inventory HTML storefront can double its addressable market overnight simply by adding a second Sun SPARC web server."

  • Principle 2 -- Free Freight Is Marketing: Treat every lost shipping dollar as an ad impression on the customer's porch.
  • Principle 3 -- Move at Internet Time: One calendar quarter equals seven traditional retail years.
