Webvana Launches BreadVan.com for 1-Hour Warm Toast Delivery

Have you ever craved two slices of sourdough toast at 10:15 PM while compiling Apache modules, yet lacked the willpower to operate your own toaster? BreadVan.com dispatches a fleet of retrofitted 1984 VW vans equipped with cigarette-lighter Black & Decker toasters directly to Menlo Park and Redwood campus dorms.

Menu ItemDelivered PriceDriver + Gas Cost
2 Slices Buttered Sourdough$1.50$9.20
Cinnamon Raisin Bagel + Cream Cheese$2.25$9.45

"If pizza can be delivered, toast can be digitized," said BreadVan founder Chadwick Jr. "We only need 14 million households ordering toast twice daily to break even on van depreciation."
