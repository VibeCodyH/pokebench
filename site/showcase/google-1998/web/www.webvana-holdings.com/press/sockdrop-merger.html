SockDrop.com Acquires ToeWarmerNet in $38M All-Stock Swap

In a landmark consolidation of the online hosiery sector, Webvana-backed SockDrop.com today absorbed rival ToeWarmerNet in an all-stock transaction valued at $38 million -- or approximately $950 per registered sock subscriber.

  • Combined Catalog: Over 410 SKUs of crew socks, argyle dress socks, and thermal boot liners.
  • Synergies: Merging both Perl CGI shopping carts cuts duplicate 468x60 banner spend by $180,000/month.
  • Free Single-Sock Replacement: Lose one sock in the dryer? SockDrop ships a single mate overnight free!
