Gamer Briefing

Collecting all 48 Hidden Time Shards unlocks the secret True Ending: The Restored Dawn and lets Kael keep the ChronoBlade Omega in New Game+! Here is the region checklist.


48 Time Shard Regional Breakdown
RegionShardsHardest One to Find

Whispering Fen & Swamp Ruins#13 - #24#19: Use Lantern Relic in the fog maze (North, North, West, South)
Sunken Clockwork Spire#25 - #36#31: Ride the reverse gear teeth into the ceiling shaft (see Wall-Clip FAQ!)
Astral Rift & Forge#37 - #48#48: Defeat optional shadow boss Pale Knight inside Void Shrine
