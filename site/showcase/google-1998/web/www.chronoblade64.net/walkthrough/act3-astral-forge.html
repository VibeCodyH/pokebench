Gamer Briefing

Act III Endgame Walkthrough: Enter the Astral Forge at Level 42+ with 10 Chrono-Elixirs and Astral Aegis equipped. Save on Slot 2 before stepping onto the obsidian dais -- there is no going back once the portal seals!


Final Boss Phases: Lord Aethelgard
PhaseHP & AttacksSurvival Strategy
Phase 1: Architect of Ruin14,000 HP | Gravity CrushKeep Temporal Haste up on Lyra; use Thorne's Shield Wall whenever Aethelgard charges his staff.
Phase 2: Eternity Wyrm22,500 HP | Shattered TimelineShattered Timeline drops all party members to 1 HP unless you defend! Immediately pop a Mega-Elixir and spam ChronoBlade Omega Finishers.
