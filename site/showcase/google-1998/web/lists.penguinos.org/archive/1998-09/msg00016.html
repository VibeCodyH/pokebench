Tested three lab PC motherboards for the 1999->2000 clock rollover using `hwclock`.
A 1996 Award 4.51PG BIOS rolled straight back to 1900-01-01 after power-cycling at
23:59:50 on Dec 31, 1999! Linux booted with Jan 1 1970 until `ntpdate` corrected it.

# hwclock --set --date="12/31/1999 23:59:50"
# sleep 15 && hwclock --show
Sat Jan  1 00:00:05 1900  -0.412890 seconds   <-- BIOS Y2K BUG!
