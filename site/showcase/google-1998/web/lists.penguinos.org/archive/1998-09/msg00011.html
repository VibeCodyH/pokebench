> modprobe sb fails with Device or resource busy unless isapnp runs first...

Run `pnpdump > /etc/isapnp.conf` first and inspect every `(INT 0 ...)` line.
Most SoundBlaster AWE64 boards need `(DMA 0 (CHANNEL 1))`, `(DMA 1 (CHANNEL 5))`,
and `modprobe awe_wave` after `sb.o` for MIDI wavetable synthesis.

# pnpdump -c > /etc/isapnp.conf
# /sbin/isapnp /etc/isapnp.conf && /sbin/modprobe sb
