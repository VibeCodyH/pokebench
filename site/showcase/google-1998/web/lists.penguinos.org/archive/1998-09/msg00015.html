> USRobotics Sportster 56k V.90 logs Modem hangup after 20 minutes...

Copper phone lines with noisy bridge taps fail V.90 retraining after ~20 minutes.
Lock your init string to `AT&F1S32=34` to disable V.90 and force rock-solid K56flex
or 33.6k V.34, plus add `lcp-echo-interval 30` to `/etc/ppp/options`.

OK
AT&F1S32=34
CONNECT 44000/ARQ/K56/LAPM/V42BIS
