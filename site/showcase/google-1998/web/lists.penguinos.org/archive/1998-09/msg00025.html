Our Borealis mirror guestbook script gets slammed whenever Slashdot or Freshmeat
links a new ISO release. Apache 1.3.1 spawns 80 httpd children and hits MaxClients
because KeepAliveTimeout is still at the default 15 seconds for 56k dialup clients.

# /etc/httpd/conf/httpd.conf
KeepAlive On
KeepAliveTimeout 4
MaxKeepAliveRequests 30
MinSpareServers 8
