> ppa0: failed to negotiate EPP mode, falling back to Nibble!

Reboot into your motherboard BIOS Setup -> Integrated Peripherals -> Parallel Port
Mode and switch from `SPP` or `ECP` to **`EPP 1.9`**! In Nibble mode the Zip 100
creeps at 40 KB/s and drops IRQ7 under heavy SCSI load.

ppa: Parport 0x378 running in EPP 32-bit mode -> 820 KB/s sustained
