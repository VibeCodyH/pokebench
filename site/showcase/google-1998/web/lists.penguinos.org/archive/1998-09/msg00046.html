Amen. Never pass unsanitized CGI parameters to `open(MAIL, "|$cmd")` or raw
two-argument `open()`. Use `open(MAIL, "|/usr/sbin/sendmail -t -oi")` with zero
shell interpolation and `flock()` on guestbook data files.

open(MAIL, "|-") || exec('/usr/sbin/sendmail', '-t', '-oi');
