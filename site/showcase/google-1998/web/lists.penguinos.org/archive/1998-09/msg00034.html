> Dialup users get 550 Relaying denied from sendmail 8.9.1...

Don't re-open promiscuous relaying -- spammers will abuse it within 24 hours.
Enable POP-before-SMTP (`popauth` hashing recent POP3 logins into `/etc/mail/access.db`)
or instruct SSH users to tunnel port 25 / read via `fetchmail` + Pine locally.

# /etc/mail/access
128.32.44	RELAY
# popauth daemon adds transient /32 dialup IPs for 15 minutes
