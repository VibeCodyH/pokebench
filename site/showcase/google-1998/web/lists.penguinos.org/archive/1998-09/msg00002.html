> Seeing hard lockups on our Tyan Tiger 100 dual PII-400 test box under 2.1.120 SMP.

Try booting with `noapic` first, or enable the APIC NMI watchdog timer so it dumps
an EIP trace to the VGA console instead of silently hanging inside spin_lock_irqsave.
Also check if you compiled with UP_APIC disabled -- on 440BX dual boards IO-APIC
IRQ routing for SCSI + Tulip NICs collides without `pirq=10,11,9`.

lilo: linux apic_watchdog=1 debug=2
