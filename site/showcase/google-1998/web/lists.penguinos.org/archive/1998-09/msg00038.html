> Compiled egcs 1.1b and tested -O3 -mpentiumpro on C++ indexer...

WARNING: Do NOT compile Linux 2.0.35 kernels with `egcs -O6` or even `egcs -O2`!
egcs enforces strict aliasing and breaks `outb()` / `memcpy` inline assembly in
2.0.x string headers. Keep `gcc 2.7.2.3` as `CC` for building 2.0.35 kernels!

# linux-2.0.35/Makefile:
CC = /usr/bin/gcc272 -D__KERNEL__ -I$(HPATH)
