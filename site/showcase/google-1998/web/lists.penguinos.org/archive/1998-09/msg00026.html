> Apache 1.3.1 spawns 80 httpd children hitting MaxClients on guestbook CGI...

Tuning `KeepAliveTimeout 4` helps socket reuse, but fork/exec overhead of launching
`/usr/bin/perl` on every CGI hit is your real bottleneck. Move the guestbook under
`mod_perl` (`Apache::Registry`) and watch latency drop from 180ms to 12ms.

Benchmark: 14.2 req/s (plain CGI) -> 182.6 req/s (mod_perl 1.15 Apache::Registry)
