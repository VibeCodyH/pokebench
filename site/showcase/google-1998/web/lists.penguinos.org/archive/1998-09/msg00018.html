> Linux time_t itself won't overflow until Jan 19, 2038...

Watch out in userland Perl 5 CGI scripts though! Half the hit counters and guestbooks
on campus write `"19$year"` after `($sec,$min,$hr,$mday,$mon,$year) = localtime()`,
which prints `19100` on January 1, 2000! Always use `$year += 1900;`.

my ($year) = (localtime)[5];
$year += 1900;  # 100 + 1900 = 2000, NOT "19$year"!
