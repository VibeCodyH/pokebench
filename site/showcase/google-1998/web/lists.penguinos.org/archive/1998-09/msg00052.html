> Xircom CreditCard 10/100 + 56k combo card triggers IRQ 3 clash...

Add `exclude irq 3` and `exclude irq 4` to `/etc/pcmcia/config.opts` if built-in
COM1/COM2 are enabled in BIOS, forcing the Xircom NIC onto IRQ 9 or 10 and the
internal PCMCIA modem onto ttyS2.

# /etc/pcmcia/config.opts
exclude irq 3
exclude irq 4
include port 0x100-0x4ff, irq 9, 10, 11
