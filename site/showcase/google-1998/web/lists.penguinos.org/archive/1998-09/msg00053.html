Woke up to `/var` at 100% capacity on our 2GB Quantum SCSI drive! Turns out
`logrotate` renamed `/var/log/messages` to `messages.1` without sending SIGHUP
to `syslogd`, so syslog kept writing 800MB to an unlinked file descriptor.

# lsof | grep deleted
syslogd  194  root  4w  REG  8,3  842109440  (deleted) /var/log/messages.1
