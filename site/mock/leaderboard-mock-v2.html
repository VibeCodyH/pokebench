The ground rules

LLMs play Pokémon.

A big brain meets a small directional pad. Suite 1 is Pokémon Red (Gen 1). Every model gets the same 1,000-turn budget and is scored by the furthest milestone it reaches. More generations may be added as the benchmark grows.

Gen 1: the eight milestones.

  1. 1Left house
  2. 2Route 1
  3. 3Got starter
  4. 4Viridian City
  5. 5Viridian Forest
  6. 6Pewter City
  7. 7Brock’s Gym
  8. 8Beat Brock

The route maps are schematic. Numbered checkpoints show benchmark order, not a game walkthrough.

Progress is the score.

  • Reach milestone 5? Score 5/8. Beat Brock? 8/8. Equal milestones share a rank.
  • The run ends at the turn limit or when the final milestone is reached. A red X marks the furthest checkpoint of an unfinished run.
  • Tokens, cost, and time are reported, not scored. Spending more doesn’t buy leaderboard points.
  • LOCAL and API runs use the same milestone ladder. “FREE” means no API spend; local hardware and electricity aren’t included.
  • Future suites can define their own games and milestones. Compare scores within a suite.
Red is the first stop.

More generations may follow. The premise stays the same: let the models play.
