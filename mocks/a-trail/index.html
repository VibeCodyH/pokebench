    THE RULES OF THIS VERY SMALL MOUNTAIN

    Big brains. Same buttons.

    LLMs play Pokémon Red on a 1,000-turn budget. Score is the furthest of ten milestones reached; beating Brock is the ceiling. Ties go to fewer turns to reach that milestone. Tokens, cost and time are reported, not scored. Tap any name tag to read its run, then flip it to follow the journey.

    The ten-rung ladder, including the trip back to Oak

      Places group the run's final milestone. Getting the Pokédex puts a run back in Pallet Town, even though it has already visited Viridian City.
